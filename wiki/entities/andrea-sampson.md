## Her frameworks — HERS, fence them

- **What an advertising strategist actually does** — *"we're **the voice of the consumer inside the
  agency**"*; research synthesised down to *"two, maybe three insights, and **an insight would be nothing
  more than a sentence**"*, which becomes the creative brief. ★ Her aside: *"**insights are often boring —
  it's creative that actually brings them to life.** … **We were the unsung heroes.**"*
- **Idea-centric storytelling** — *"**any good TED Talk is an idea that's wrapped in story.**"* Listen for
  the *mechanism* inside the idea, then find a personal story that works as a **metaphor** for it, using
  details that are *"transcendent across time and space."*
- **The tell-it-back technique** — after an hour or so, *"**I tell it back to them as if I am them**… **for
  the first time somebody understood them**, and **they've never heard their own story told back to
  them.**"*
- **The E*Trade Canada pitch story** (~2001) — the *"get-rich-quicks"* vs *"aggressive affluent"*
  segmentation, the *beat your broker* insight, and the **$1.79 squeegee** punchline.
- **Her positioning answer** — *"**it's actually your life and the context of your life that makes you
  different**… **it doesn't matter that a million other people are saying the same thing — the way you say
  it, because of who you are, means somebody else understands it in a way they never could have.**"*
- ★ **A line worth attributing to her**: *"**we teach what we most need to learn.**"*

## ⚠️ Handling personal material

She discloses her own history — 25 years in advertising, the decision at 50 (*"**it never felt like a
crisis, it felt like an awakening**"*), a hair-colour confrontation with an HR director, dropping out in
grade 10, and **a neurodiversity disclosure.** **Hers, on her own terms, recorded minimally and not to be
elaborated.**

⚠️⚠️ **Her CLIENTS' stories** — a geneticist's bereavement, a São Paulo entrepreneur's childhood
homelessness, an account of witnessing violence in apartheid-era South Africa — are **third parties'
personal tragedies, related by her.** They are recorded **by category only on the source page and are never
retold.**
