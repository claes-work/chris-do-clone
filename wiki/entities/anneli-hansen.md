# Anneli Hansen — CONTEXT / FRIEND

> ⚠️ **Context page, not persona data.** Anneli Hansen is a friend and professional peer of Chris Do's, not
> the subject of this clone. Her story is recorded because **Chris uses it as a teaching case** and because
> she is the direct cause of a significant piece of The Futur's history.

**Who.** A friend of Chris's, based in Scandinavia (Stockholm is named as her city). Surname established
from [[../sources/2024-08-20-yt-ufQErCvUQnc]]; earlier sources give only "Anneli", with captions
rendering it "analie" / "anley" / "anal Hansen".

## Why she has a page

★★ **She caused the European tour.** *"It was really spurred on by my friend **Anneli Hansen**, who's like:
**I would really like for you to come to Stockholm — and can we just chain a bunch of cities together so
that it would be worth your while to come out here?**"* That request became the eight-or-nine-city tour
documented on [[the-futur]] and in [[../topics/business]] §53.
([[../sources/2024-08-20-yt-ufQErCvUQnc]])

★★ **She is the subject of his longest teaching case about vulnerability.** Told at length, with her
knowledge — *"I've shared this story with her in the room, so I don't feel wrong about this"*:

- Her difficulty was cultural: *"one of the things she struggles with **is to talk about what she wants** —
  as a woman, *I want to do these things* — **because in her culture it's wrong for her to say [that]**…
  it feels selfish, it feels wrong, and she can't admit it."*
- What she posted instead: *"**I'm confident, I'm brave, I'm a strong woman, I'm a lioness**… because that
  is socially what people in our country want to say."*
- Chris's read, and the friction it caused: *"I look at her posts and I'm like — **you're hiding. This is
  not authentic to the person that I know. There are two sides to you.** And **she gets really upset at me,
  she's angry, she's calling me names. There's a lot of friction in our relationship.**"*
- ★★ **Three years later** she posted the vulnerable version — *"vulnerable, anxious, conflicted, **an
  imperfect human**"* — asking first, *"**I'm afraid to post it. Will it work, Chris?**"* His answer:
  *"there's only one way to find out. **I cannot guarantee anything, but you've got to hit send.**"*
- The result, in her words back to him: *"**the amount of love, support, what people feel about me, the
  confidence they see in me by me being vulnerable — who knew this would happen, Chris?**"*
- ★ **His response is the teaching point**, and it is characteristically two-edged: *"I don't want to apply
  that judgement. **Everybody has their own timeline. It could be 10 years, it could be 10 days.**
  **It takes as long as it takes.**"* — followed immediately by *"the way that I stab her a little bit more
  is I say: **imagine where you would have been if you had done this three years ago.**"*
  ([[../sources/2024-03-14-yt-0wse5TRJYHE]])

★ **Independent corroboration of his three-traits framework.** Ahead of the European tour he asked her for
her three traits of magnetic people: *"she said this, this and this — **I'm like, oh my God, our list is
exactly the same. Exactly the same.**"* He offers it as evidence, not decoration.
([[../sources/2024-06-28-yt-OFoklpI-ndc]])

## Handling note

Her story is told **with her consent** and in service of a teaching point, which is consistent with Chris's
own **personal-story rule** (`content-strategy` §45). The persona may use it the same way — as a case about
**timelines and courage**, not as gossip about a named person — and should keep his own two-edged framing
(*it takes as long as it takes* **and** *imagine where you'd be*), since removing either half misrepresents
him.
