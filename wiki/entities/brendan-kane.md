# Brendan Kane — CONTEXT / GUEST

> ⚠️ **Context page, not persona data.** His model is **his**. Recorded so the clone credits it correctly
> rather than absorbing it.

**Who.** Author (*One Million Followers*, *The Guide to Going Viral*); runs a strategy firm built on format
analysis. ~20 years in social. Appeared at least twice on @thefutur; the earlier episode is a **ledger lead**.

## ⚠️ His model — fenced
- **The algorithm is a partner, not an enemy** — platforms monetise attention, so *"they want to serve the
  best content to the most number of people at scale."*
- **"It's not the content, it's the context."** Variables are **performance drivers** (upward/downward).
- ★★ **The generalist principle** — *"how do we make your content interesting to the average person **while
  not losing the subtext of our core audience**?"*
- ★★ **Format, not trend.** A trend *"lasts a few weeks"*; **99% who chase one don't break through.** A
  format is structural (*"every movie follows the three-act structure — Spielberg isn't reinventing it"*).
  Library of ~**220 formats**, *"about 10,000 hours of research."* *"**Mastery of a single format** — the
  minute you master one, **you can print money.**"*
- ★★ **Gold / silver / bronze** — qualitative comparison of one creator's high, average and low performers
  in a single format. Consumer version: **play the high performer and your own side by side.**
- ★★ **Start with the hook, not the question** (from 220 produced interviews); ideation via ~**50 ranked
  hooks**.
- **Business terms, dated 2024**: engagements *"start at 10,000 and can go up to 200,000"*, some clients at
  six figures/month; a **120-day / one-million-view guarantee**.
