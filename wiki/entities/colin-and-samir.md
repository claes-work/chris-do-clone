# Colin & Samir — CONTEXT / GUESTS

> ⚠️ **Context page.** Their history and frameworks are **theirs**.

**Who.** A creator-economy show; ~13 years on YouTube; built and sold a lacrosse media company (at **23**),
now interview large creators.

## ⚠️ Their material — fenced
- ★★ *"**YouTube is not creating content *for* an audience — it's creating content *with* an audience.**"*
- ★★ **The two questions**: *"**who is this for, and why would they watch?**"* — with the confession that
  they forgot to ask it for years: *"**they were for us**, not empathetic towards a broader audience."*
- ★★ **Three tracked rules**: click-through → first-7s/first-30s retention (*"I need to give you a return on
  that investment"*); **average view duration**; *"do you want more stuff like this."*
- ★★ **Creative vs. Creator**: *"**a creative does a creative act and produces creative work. A Creator is a
  distribution platform.** … **And you can be both.**"*
- ★★ **On fear**: *"**when we found success, the fear of losing it is more stifling and paralysing than the
  fear when you're trying to build something for the first time.**"*
- ★ **Partnership**: shared taste; deferring to *"who cares more"*; *"when I put my one in and my creative
  partner puts in the other one, **for some reason it equals three.**"* Against a borrowed maxim
  (*companies are sports teams, not families*) they note they operate like a family anyway.
