## Her frameworks — HERS, fence them

**None of the following may enter `persona/beliefs.md`, `persona/voice.md`, or `persona/system-prompt.md`.**

- **Empathy hijacking** — *"in an effort to connect, you tell me a story… and I say *oh my gosh, I know
  exactly how you feel because three years ago…* — **and now all of a sudden you're not even talking
  anymore. I am de-centring the narrative from you to me.**"* Including the *"well, at least…"* form.
- **Weaponizing empathy** — *"using empathy **as an excuse to inflict pain**, but also **as an excuse to
  take advantage of someone because of their empathy**."* ★ She flags it as rare: *"**those are
  outliers.**"*
- **Empathy in joy** — her game-show example: *"when someone else wins, I get a high… **I'm not on the game
  show, but I'm experiencing it as if I could feel it myself.**"*
- **Clarity as empathy** — a pillar of her book: *"**it doesn't leave people in the dark**, fumbling around
  going *where am I? What's expected of me?*"*
- ★ **Her own disclaimer, worth keeping as hers**: *"**I might be an empathy expert, but I'm not an expert
  AT empathy.**"*

## ⚠️ Handling her personal history

She uses **her account of surviving a ruptured brain aneurysm (2008) and the recovery that followed** to
illustrate empathy hijacking — people telling her they *"know exactly how you feel"* about memory loss.
**That is her disclosure about herself, on her own terms.** It is summarised **by category only** on the
source page, **is not persona data, and is never to be retold, elaborated, or used to characterise her.**
