# Matthew Encina — CONTEXT / ALUMNUS

> ⚠️ **Context page, not persona data.** Encina is not the subject of this clone. **His** story, numbers and
> frameworks are his. He is here because he is central to two things in Chris's own history.

**Who.** Chris's student at **Art Center, 2006** → intern → freelancer → **creative director at
[[blind|Blind]] for ~a decade** (work for Xbox, Coldplay) → **head of content at [[the-futur|The Futur]] from
2016** → left ≈**2021**. Now runs his own YouTube channel and works in **industrial product design** (desk
accessories, keyboards).

## ★★★ Why he matters to the clone

**1. He proposed the pivot that made The Futur the main business.** Chris credits it to him directly:
*"you've been pretty instrumental in shaping a couple of big seismic changes within my own thinking… you're
like: **Chris, what if we just focus on doing just the Futur? No more Blind service work.** And it took me a
beat. I'm like: **okay, let's try.**"* The arithmetic Chris then ran is recorded on [[the-futur]] §pass 15.

**2. He is the occasion for Chris's most exposed statement about alumni.** *"Sometimes people leave, or are
asked to leave, and **I don't ever talk to them again — and it makes me wonder a lot about: was there more
in the relationship than I thought, or was there less?** … **Unless I betrayed you, or was disloyal, or did
something unethical, why shouldn't we have a relationship?**"* — set against the counter-example: *"people
always wondered what happened to Matthew — **I just had a sandwich or taco with him the other day.**"*

**3. He recommended the headphones** that produced Chris's *"if you let out a sigh, I need to hear it"*
listening material ([[../sources/2024-12-03-yt-Vu4l1CluzCU]]). ✅ *This page resolves the caption garble
"Matthew and Cena" on that source.*

## ⚠️ His material — fenced

- **The channel**: started **early 2019** while still at The Futur; a home-office remodel video hit
  ~**100,000 views in a week** and ~**a million in three months** — against Futur content he thought was far
  more valuable (*"I would pay for this information"*). Follow-ups *"all hit a few million views each"*.
- **Leaving**: his goal was only to match his salary; *"that **first year I was making four times my salary
  in revenue.**"* Cadence then ~**one video/month with a sponsor**; now ~**one every three months** —
  *"**I don't feel stressed to constantly feed this content machine.**"*
- **Production**: a 10-minute video takes *"**at least a month, probably three**"*; product videos document
  **6–12 months** of manufacturing. He runs several projects *"in a relay race"* to avoid stalling.
- **Temperament**: *"**I'm a very risk-averse person**"*; *"**fear and excitement feel the same in your body**
  — how you look at it determines which one you're actually feeling"*; and the **dark-room-with-a-flashlight**
  metaphor for unfamiliar territory.
- **Deal structure**: enters as a designer, sometimes narrow scope, sometimes carte blanche; *"where **I'm
  taking a lot more risk, that's where I would get more of the pie**"* — typically a **royalty** across
  production runs.
