# Raw Materials — CONTEXT / COMPANY

> ⚠️ **Context page.** The company's philosophy is **its founder's**, not Chris's.

**What.** A creative company relaunched from the Austin agency **Handsome** (founded late 2011/early 2012,
grown to **140–150 people**; clients included FedEx, Visa, Audi, Meta). Stripped down and relaunched as
**Raw Materials in May 2023** after a ~2–2.5 year process; today ~**30–40 FTE**, fully distributed.
Introduced with **D&AD Agency of the Year** and **five Cannes awards**.

> ⚠️ **FOUNDER'S NAME NOT ASSERTED.** The caption track renders it two conflicting ways. Verify before
> creating a person entity. Two colleagues are consistently captioned and safe to record: **Pablo Marquez**
> (Chief Creative Officer) and **Jennifer Allen** (COO, previously R/GA).

## ⚠️ Their principles — fenced
- *"**Business should only exist to the extent that it can generate demand for the thing that it sets out to
  do and be.**"*
- *"**No idea is ever as good as when it is first conceived**"* — realising it puts it at risk; **scaling it
  really puts it at risk.**
- The single stated principle: *"**when everything is the same, different is the greatest opportunity.**"*
  Operationally: *"**find the best talent in the world and enable them to do the best work of their
  lives.**"*
- *"**All business is an experiment**… so what is the hypothesis, and the whole point is to prove it."*
