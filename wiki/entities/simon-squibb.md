# Simon Squibb — CONTEXT / GUEST

> ⚠️⚠️ **Context page, not persona data.** Squibb's life story, business model and opinions are
> **his**. This page exists so the clone can talk *about* him accurately without absorbing him. Nothing
> here may inform `persona/`.

**Who.** Creator known for street interviews asking strangers *"what's your dream?"*, then promoting
and often funding them. Self-described at the time of recording: ~15M followers, ~half a billion views
a month, 18 companies started, 80+ startup investments, *"now on my 90th business."* Author of
**What's Your Dream?**

## How he and Chris met

At the **1 Billion Followers Summit in Dubai** (early 2025), Chris noticed a crowd around a man he
couldn't identify. On a later day, riding down an escalator, the same man walked out opposite him —
*"He goes, 'Chris.'"* Squibb said *"maybe this was meant to happen,"* gave him the book, and they
agreed to make content together. Chris was in **Singapore** the day after the summit's award night,
where he saw clips of Squibb winning the **$1,000,000 prize** in tears. The interview was recorded in
Los Angeles at the house Squibb's ten-person team was staying in.
([[../sources/2025-05-22-yt-PjP5wvemDLo]])

## ⚠️ His account — fenced, summarised only for context

- Father died and his mother put him out at **15**; **8 weeks on the street** in England. Couldn't get
  benefits, a job, or successfully beg. Started a **gardening business** by knocking on doors,
  borrowing homeowners' unused equipment, and taking deposits to buy supplies.
- Moved to **Hong Kong at 23** (1997) with ~**£80,000**; slept on a friend's sofa to avoid rent. Built
  **Fluid**, a Hong Kong creative agency (~200 people incl. contract), co-founded with **Helen
  Griffith**, now his wife. **Sold it to PwC at 40** after installing a management team — *"if you end
  up being the only person that can run it, it's very hard to sell it."* Amount under NDA.
- Post-exit he describes falling into *"neither want nor need, which is even more dangerous… basically
  you're lost,"* two years off, some depression, then: *"**create a purpose that creates the need.**"*
- **The "business of giving" model as he lays it out**: (1) *"act like you've got no money"* —
  *"money makes you weak"*; (2) a **core belief** (his: the education system is broken) —
  *"**don't manage a team, manage a purpose**; hire people on that purpose and they manage
  themselves"*; (3) an **income model tied to the giving**. Stages: AdSense → brand deals → own
  products.
- On judging recipients: *"**I really try to remove judgment.** It's not about whether they're a good
  person or not. It's really about hope."* And: *"the real value isn't the money I give people —
  **it's the exposure**, the chance I give their dream to live in the world."*

## Where Chris agrees, and where he pushes back

This is the useful part of the page — it isolates *Chris's* positions without adopting Squibb's.

- **Agrees, forcefully**, on the do-good/profit asymmetry: *"I do not understand why we think if you
  do good in the world, you must do it for the goodness of doing it — **whereas if you do anything
  else for profit, no one even bats an eyelash at how much money you make.**"*
- **Disagrees on the mechanism** of online hostility. Squibb explains it by tribal DNA; Chris says
  *"I don't know if I agree with that"* and offers instead: *"we're very suspicious of people trying
  to do good in the world… **everyone starts with the knives out first before they open their hand**…
  so **it's upon you and not upon them to show up consistently to reveal your character.**"*
- **Disagrees on pure gift vs. reciprocity** — his **social capital** counter-argument (*"there's
  still a trade there; that's a community trade"*) is recorded at
  [[../topics/business/business|business §71]].
- **Presses him on the giving model** with the **C-vs-E** distinction he invents on the spot
  ([[../topics/business/business|business §72]]).
- **Names his privilege and then sets it aside — including himself**: *"You're a white guy knocking on
  doors, doors open… I just want to put that out there because people are going to say that. **But
  here's where it's not easy: a lot of us wouldn't knock on those doors. I put myself in that
  category.**"*
- **Refuses the obvious money question**: asked to ask *"how do you justify spending $50,000?"* —
  *"**I would never ask that… Small-minded question.** It doesn't matter how much you spend. It
  matters how much money you make or take."*
- **Does not concede on passionate detachment.** Squibb objects to it directly (*"it's like saying
  don't be too much in love because when you break up you might cry"*); Chris presses the
  inconsistency rather than yielding. ⚠️ **Recorded as an open tension.**
