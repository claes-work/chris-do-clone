# Stefan Sagmeister — CONTEXT / INFLUENCE

> ⚠️ **Context page.** Present in the corpus as **one half of a comparison Chris built**, not as a guest.

Around **2017–18** Chris presented to the Futur Pro community on *"two people in the design community with
equally really strong personal brands **who could not be more different**"* — Sagmeister and
[[aaron-draplin|Draplin]].

| Sagmeister, as Chris characterises him | Draplin |
|---|---|
| *"Austrian, very proper designer, whose work is exhibited in museums, has written many books… **part of that elite design class**"* | one employee, no awards, no book |
| Judges award shows; sabbaticals; *"deep ideas and observations about art history and philosophy"* | *"droppin' F bombs"* |
| Terrorised by stray dogs while travelling → made a t-shirt: *"**so many dogs, so few recipes**"* | his chihuahua: *"**what's good for his mouth is good for my mouth**"* |
| *"An international citizen, a citizen of the world"* | *"**my friends are saving to go to India… but I go to Indiana.**"* |

★★ **Chris's conclusion, which is the transferable part**: *"**they're probably not sharing the same fan
base, but they're both powerful people.** We knew who they were, we knew what they stood for, **we knew what
they stood against.**"*
