> ✅ **Attribution:** Chris teaching his own method, first person. One of the **earliest sources in the
> corpus** (2014, the Skool era).

## Summary

★★★★★ **Stylescapes are among his most identifiable deliverables** — [[../topics/branding/branding|branding]]
carries them as **Framework 22**, with no provenance. **Here he gives the provenance himself, in two steps,
and says outright that he did not invent it.**

## ★★★★★ Chris-attributed material

### ★★★★★ ⭐ The provenance chain, and the disclaimer

> ★★★★★ *"There's something called **STYLE TILES**, which you can also research — it's very similar, pretty
> much almost the same thing with a slight exception, and **we make ours a little bit more artfully.**
> **I LEARNED THIS FROM HENRY MINH, AND HE GOT IT FROM DIGITAS.** **You just got to give reference to where
> you got it — I DIDN'T INVENT IT.**"*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Prior art he names** | **style tiles** — `style tiles` returns **0** corpus-wide |
| **Who taught him** | ⚠️ **"Henry Minh"** (caption spelling; may be *Ming*/*Min* — **not verified**) — **0** corpus-wide |
| **Where that person got it** | **Digitas** (the agency) — **0** corpus-wide |
| **His own contribution** | *"we make ours a little bit more **artfully**"* |

✅ **Three of the four elements are absent from the corpus entirely**, and the fourth — his own contribution
— is the only part it currently records.

> ⚠️⚠️ **This is a rule-6 exposure.** `branding.md` **Framework 22** presents stylescapes as his method.
> **The earliest source we have has him naming prior art, naming the person who taught him, naming where
> that person got it, and stating *"I didn't invent it."*** ⚠️ **The corpus is not wrong that he refined and
> popularised it — it is missing that he says he did not originate it.**

📌 **Note also the meta-instruction inside the quote:** *"**you just got to give reference to where you got
it**."* **He is not only crediting a source, he is prescribing the practice of crediting** — which makes the
omission in the hub the opposite of his own stated standard.

⚠️ **What I could NOT verify:** the spelling of *"Henry Minh"*, whether he is the same person as the
**Henry Kaminsky** already on **12** corpus pages (**probably not** — different surname), and what exactly
Digitas' original artefact was. **All three recorded as open.**

### ★★★ The workflow point it sits inside

a lot of friction."* — the stylescape exists to occupy that gap. ✅ Consistent with Framework 22's
*"deliberately don't look like the final work"* framing.
