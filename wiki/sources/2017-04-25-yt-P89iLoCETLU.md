## ★★★★★ ⭐⭐ ROLAND YOUNG — a formative teacher at 0 corpus-wide

> ✅ `Roland Young` returns **0**, and the stem `Roland` also returns **0**. **The corpus does not contain
> this person.** He is the teacher of Chris's **first class at ArtCenter**.

> ★★★★★ *"First class I walk into happened to be **COMMUNICATION DESIGN ONE**, and it was taught by
> **ROLAND YOUNG.** On your transcript it just says **R. Young** — I didn't know the R was for Roland."*

> ★★★★★ *"This **elderly man with shocking white hair** walks in. Black sweatshirt, black sweatpants,
> beat-up sneakers — **he looks like a homeless guy.** …And **he goes on to blow my mind.**"*

> ★★★★★ *"A couple of classes in, **I called up my mom** — it was like a 16-year-old schoolgirl —
> **mom, my teacher, he's a GENIUS. I can't believe it. He's a true genius.** …**She's never heard me say
> that word. I've never even said that word. I didn't know what a genius WAS. And that guy was a genius.**"*

✅ `he's a genius` returns **0**. ⚠️ **Note what he is describing** — not a lesson but a **recalibration of
a word**. 📌 **Family: role only** (*"my mom"*), no name — per the standing rule.

### ★★★★ His critique method, and the fear

> ★★★★ *"When we would do our designs, he would just look at it — and **in a split second** Roland Young
> would look at it and say: **do you mean THIS?** And he would just go like that to your work."*

> ★★★★ *"**We all feared him — because he EARNED our respect.** …He pulled out these ideas just out of
> the universe. Even though he was in his fifties and I'm in my twenties, **I thought this man has more
> vibrant, fresh, innovative thinking than the 20-year-olds in this group.**"*

📌 *"He came in every single week with the exact same clothes on. **He wore his uniform.**"*
⚠️ **A verifiable pointer he gives the audience:** *"go and search on the internet — **Roland is God** —
and you will see a video of this man **tearing people apart ruthlessly.**"* ✅ **Useful for confirming the
name**; not followed up here.

### ★★★★★ ⭐ The lesson he still quotes 25 years later

> ★★★★★ *"**I remember it to this day, over 22 years later, almost 25 years now.** He said: **who are you
> guys competing against when you're in this class?** People said: with each other, with the department,
> with that other school. And he goes: **no. You're going to be competing against ME.** *[pointing]*
> **Are you going to be able to take work away from me?**"*

> ★★★★★ *"And that was reality. I worship you, you're dropping conceptual bombs on me — and you're telling
> me now **I've got to compete with you. YOU'VE GOT TO SLAY THE MASTER.**"*

✅ `slay the master` returns **0**. ⚠️ **This reframes the teacher–student relation as future competition**,
and Chris presents it as the thing that landed hardest. ⭐ **Strong candidate for `beliefs.md`** — it is a
formative principle he can date and attribute.

## ★★★★ Other genuinely new material

### ★★★★ ⭐ The rejections, and the family framing

> ★★★★ *"It wasn't until **I got rejected out of UCLA, UC San Diego, and Cal Poly San Luis Obispo** — and
> I had **no more options.** And that was **considered disgraceful for my family**, to say their
> **SECOND-BORN SON** couldn't get into a university one they can brag to their friends."*

✅ `second born` returns **0** — ⚠️ **birth order is new**; family role only, no names.
⚠️ 🗣️ Self-reported, and he states the cultural frame in his own blunt terms: *"where I grew up in
Northern California, everybody was going to become a computer scientist… **this is where all the washed-up
loser Asian kids go — community college.**"* ✅ He explicitly rejects the tidy version: *"**it wasn't part
of some plan**… the plan was **I just couldn't get into any other school.**"*

### ★★★★ ⭐ The silk-screening boss who named ArtCenter

> ★★★★ *"It's very serendipitous that I happened to be **working at a silk-screening place where we design
> t-shirts.** I told my boss… he goes: **the best school you're ever going to go to for graphic design is
> a school called ART CENTER** — **this is the Harvard of design schools.**"*

✅ `silk screening` returns **0**. ⚠️ **He is candid that the choice was arbitrary**: *"**You could have
said 'go to butthole design' and I would have said: that's it, I'm going to butthole design.**"*
📌 **Two more people then validated it** — his **high school yearbook teacher** (whom he had previously
rebuffed: *"I'm going to be broke, that's not a thing for me, this is not cool"*), and later his brother.

### ★★★★ The brother's intervention — and the credit card

> ★★★★ *"**My brother's always looked out for me.** He said: **come live with me, go to college here. You
> got to get away from mom and dad. You got to figure out your way in life.**"* ⚠️ Chris upgrades the
> characterisation himself: *"that sounds like big-brother advice — **this is more than big brother, this
> is fatherly advice.**"*
> ⭐ *"**He's given me a credit card so I can start my business.**"* ⚠️ 🗣️ Self-reported; **role only, no name.**

📌 The conclusion he draws: *"**people around you can see things about you that you're kind of blind to.**"*

### ★★★★ Typography One — the claim, and the gallery

> ★★★★★ *"**Typography is the SECRET GLUE that unlocks all the design problems in your life.** I promise
> you: **become a master of type, you become a master of design.**"* ✅ `secret glue` returns **0**.

> ★★★★ *"When I took **type one in third term at Art Center, my life had changed at that minute.** …I'd
> walked through the student gallery like **a kid in a candy store**, thinking *one day I hope my work is
> good enough to be among these people.* After finishing typography one with **Simon Johnston** — a
> British designer, my professor — **I went back to the gallery and it looked DIFFERENT to me. It
> literally looked different.**"*

⚠️ **He supplies his own analogy and dates it**: the Matrix scene where Neo stops seeing a person and sees
code — *"**and this is before the Matrix.**"* ✅ `Simon Johnston` returns **7** incl. `biography.md` — the
professor is known; **the gallery before/after is the new part**, and so is what he concluded: *"this could
just be my sheer hubris, but I thought: **I don't want to do work that's good. I'm going to do work that's
BETTER than this, because I can do it today.**"*

### ★★★ Two provenance notes

- ⚠️ **Attributed to someone else, correctly:** *"analyse your **9pm to 12am** — are you watching TV, are
  you learning, are you sketching… **and that comes from GARY VAYNERCHUK.** He asks his people to analyse
  their nine-to-twelve all the time."* ✅ `Gary Vaynerchuk` returns **40** — well known; **the 9-to-12
  exercise is the addition.**
- ★★★ **Lettering ≠ typography**, taught by different people: *"**Nils taught lettering. He did not teach
  typography, nor did Simon teach lettering.** …You're not a typographer if you're drawing letterforms."*
  ✅ `Gemma O'Brien` (named as a lettering artist who ran a workshop there) returns **4** — already held.
