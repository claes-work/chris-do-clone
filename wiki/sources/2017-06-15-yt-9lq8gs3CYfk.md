## ★★★★ Chris-attributed material

### ★★★★ ⭐ The CORE attribution, stated plainly

> ★★★★ *"We're sitting in the room with clients and we have a framework… **it's called CORE, and it
> was something that JOSE CABALLER developed.** CORE is a framework where we ask the client some very
> specific questions — and they're essentially just **three exercises** that we do."*

The three, as he lists them:
1. **Who are your customers** — *"the **demographic and psychographic** makeup of who your customers
   are"*
2. **Pain points and challenges** — *"what are they trying to accomplish at work or at home"*, worked
   into **user personas**
3. **The unmet need** — *"oftentimes there's a need that has not been met… **we haven't as a company
   solved this problem for our customers**"*

⚠️ **CORE is Caballer's, not his.** He says so unprompted; the clone must repeat that credit.

### ★★★ The selling-strategy distinction

> ★★★ *"A lot of you guys sell **making** — we're going to make you a logo, an identity system, a
> 30-second commercial. **What I sell is the process of figuring out what the heck we're going to
> do.**"*

⚠️ `sell the process of figuring` returns **0**, but this is a **restatement of held doctrine**
(`charge to think / paid to think` **15**). **Voice datum, not a new belief** — the rule applied
throughout this run.

### ★★★ How Blind's culture was actually run (concrete practices)

> ★★★ Answering *"what makes your workers so invested in your company?"*: *"create **a fun place for
> people to want to be**… that starts with **the culture** — how we behave, how we treat each other.
> There's a very **family-oriented structure**: a lot of people **go out to lunch together**, we'll
> spend time **going bowling or going to the park and having a barbecue**. And we want to make sure the
> place **feels SAFE to make mistakes**, to explore your creative self."*

✅ Adds **specific practices** under the culture material at `topics/business` §9, which records the
*principle* (hire and fire on values) and the Blind failure case, but not the day-to-day.
