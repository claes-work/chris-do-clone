## Summary

★★★★★ **One of the most biographically dense sources in the corpus, and almost none of it is held.**
He speaks at the **Institute of Designers in Ireland (IDI) Gender Balance Initiative** in Dublin — his
first time in Ireland — about an online pile-on he walked into, and connects it to his childhood.

## ★★★★★ Chris-attributed material

### ★★★★ The framing he opens on

> ★★★★ *"I'm not sure if it's escaped you or not, but **I'm not a woman, nor a feminist, or an activist.**
> So you're probably wondering, **why am I up here? What's this guy doing?**"*

✅ He states his own lack of standing before anyone else can. ⚠️ Note the observation he brings instead of
a position: *"more than half"* of his design students are female, but *"once we enter into the workplace,
especially in the **motion design** community, there are fewer than half working inside the field and even
fewer in management or leadership roles."* ⚠️ 🗣️ **Self-reported observation, not a cited statistic.**

### ★★★★★ ⭐ The tweet and the backlash

> ★★★★★ *"I reach out to this wonderful community online and I throw this tweet up. I asked: **why aren't
> there more women in design? What's the problem, and how can we address this?** …What I didn't realise
> was **I was stepping into a political and an emotional landmine.** …**The Twitterverse exploded on my
> face.** There was tremendous backlash."*

> ★★★★★ *"**I should have said: I NEED HELP. I've been invited. I don't know what I'm doing.** And throw
> myself on the sword. **Please help me.**"*

✅ `tried to quit` returns **0**. ⚠️ **He states the correction as a change of framing, not of position** —
asking for help rather than posing the question as if he were convening the discussion.

> ★★★★ *"Women would say: **don't put the burden of learning on us. We've had enough to deal with. You as
> a man need to figure it out yourself.** And that's saying it nicely."*

✅ **He reports the criticism in the critics' own terms** and does not rebut it — recorded as said.

### ★★★★★ ⭐ "I thought I was one of the good guys" — and the near-quit

> ★★★★★ *"**I thought I was one of the good guys.** I have always felt like **I've identified more with
> women than with men. I hired women. I PAID WOMEN MORE than their male counterparts**… and I hired women
> to hold **the highest positions of power in our company. At one point, we were all women except for me.**"*

> ★★★★★ *"**I tried to quit. And this was even my idea.** Somebody direct-messaged me: **you should quit.
> You don't deserve to be there. You are taking the place of someone more deserving than you.**"*

✅ `paid women more` and `tried to quit` both return **0**. ⚠️ 🗣️ **The hiring and pay claims are
self-reported** and unverifiable from this source — marked as such, not as company record.
📌 **He notices the irony himself**: *"I'm on the internet telling everybody, **face your fears, overcome
doubt** — and here it is, **I was looking for the exit door.**"*

### ★★★★★ ⭐ The childhood, told as the reason the attack landed

_He introduces it via therapy: **"the things you react to today are kind of things that you're still trying
to deal with from your past."** ✅ Consistent with the documented therapy material._

> ★★★★★ *"**That's my mom and my dad. I'm the girl in the yellow dress.** …We're **first-generation
> immigrants. We fled Vietnam in 1975 to escape communism.** We came to the United States **when I was
> three years old** and **we left everything.**"*

> ★★★★★ *"It was really hard to make friends… **In some neighborhoods I was NOT BROWN ENOUGH, and for
> some I WASN'T WHITE ENOUGH.** And that meant I was **isolated, targeted, and picked on by bullies and
> bigots who would yell racial slurs at me. I was SPIT ON by complete strangers and I got into lots of
> fights.**"*

> ★★★★ *"**I'm not telling you I'm brave or courageous or strong**… I just was **in survival mode**, and
> unfortunately all that external pressure really started to **make me lose myself.**"*

✅ `not brown enough`, `spit on`, `yellow dress` all return **0** corpus-wide.

contradicts the same sentence's sense and his documented *"I'm not a big guy, not masculine, didn't like
sports."* **Most likely "asthmatic."** ⚠️ **Recorded as uncertain; do NOT quote this word.**
✅ **Family generic only** (*"my mom and my dad"*) — **no names**, per the standing rule.

### ★★★★ How he got back

> ★★★★ *"In my darkest moment, it was **the generosity of strangers — women who reached out to me.** They
> helped me find my way back. They would **chat with me for hours** during that very long weekend. They
> encouraged me **to tell my story, to tell my truth, and show up as myself.**"*

⭐ **One is named:** *"And one woman in particular, **Diane Gibbs**, reminded me of all the good that I was

### ★★★★ The closing observation

> ★★★★ *"**Reality did not measure up to my fears** — the audience were really warm, welcoming, friendly…
> **most of my fears were unwarranted. This is an example of where something that happens ONLINE isn't
> indicative of how it really works IN REAL LIFE.**"*
