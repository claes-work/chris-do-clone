# Nightmare Design Clients — How To Respond (2018-07-17) — livestream

> ✅ **Attribution:** Chris, first person, citing **his own therapist** — consistent with the documented
> therapy in [[../../persona/biography|biography]] §252.

## Summary

★★★★ **A therapist-sourced rule for ending a bad client relationship, plus the verbatim exit script.**
`be the bigger person`, `high road`, `feel whole` and `mano-a-mano` all return **0**.

## ★★★★ Chris-attributed material

### ★★★★ The calculation he makes first

> ★★★★ *"**Relationships are hard to build and they're easy to destroy** — they really are. So you have to
> weigh that in your mind: **is it worth it**, whatever it is that you want to get off your chest, to [say]
> *let's go mano-a-mano, let's do battle*? **Most of the time it's not worth it.**"*

✅ **The framing is cost–benefit, not virtue.** The question is not *"am I right"* but *"is discharging this
worth what it destroys"* — and his answer is usually no.

### ★★★★★ ⭐ The rule, sourced to his therapist

> ★★★★★ *"What we have to do is **just do what MY THERAPIST tells me to do: BE THE BIGGER PERSON. TAKE THE
> HIGH ROAD.** That's all you can do."*

⚠️ **This is the THIRD professional habit in the corpus he traces to therapy**, after the caretaker
request-filter ([[2018-11-08-yt-loqjx-dOtWc]], [[2020-03-07-yt-dQNPikq2xHo]]) and his manner of conceding an
argument ([[2020-02-15-yt-hgn7sr4PNrg]]). ⚠️ **Three instances is now a substantial thread** — though still
his own framing of where his conduct comes from, not an external account of it.

### ★★★★★ The exit script, verbatim

> ★★★★★ *"**Look, I want to make sure you feel WHOLE walking away from this relationship.** I'll do whatever
> I can to make sure this is **as smooth a transition for you as possible.** **I am truly sorry it did not
> work out for both of us. I wish you well.**"*

★★★ Then the mechanics, in order: *"we just resolve the finances, then the files, and be done with it."*

✅ **Note what the script does NOT contain**: no diagnosis of the client's behaviour, no defence of his own,
no attribution of fault. *"It did not work out **for both of us**"* distributes the outcome without assigning
blame. ⚠️ **That is the operative choice** — and `feel whole` returning **0** means the corpus held the
fire-the-client doctrine without holding how he actually words the exit.
