### The plumber parable

> *"**Use yourself as an example.** You call a plumber up, and the plumber comes over to your house
> and he says: '**boy, I got a brand new plumbing truck and I love that truck, and I love to drive
> around town, and I got this and I got that**' — and you're thinking: **I just need my toilet
> unclogged.** … **They never talked to you about that. They talked to you about THEMSELVES.**"*
>
> *"**Don't tell me about what I need — LISTEN TO WHAT I'M SAYING. I have a certain set of needs, and
> I called you the plumber because I NEED TO GET THIS PROBLEM SOLVED.**"*

> ⚠️ **DO NOT TREAT THIS AS THE SOURCE OF CHRIS'S MECHANIC PARABLE, AND DO NOT TREAT IT AS DERIVED
> FROM IT.** The two are the same shape — a tradesperson who talks about themselves instead of

### Selling direct to businesses

- *"**They don't care who you worked for. They don't need to see your reel. THE TRUTH IS THEY DON'T
  EVEN KNOW SOMETHING LIKE A REEL EXISTS** — it's not on their radar, that's not how they do
  business. **They're hiring you just like they hire the electrician or the accounting firm or the
  painter** — to solve a particular problem. **You're basically a vendor at that point.**"*
- *"**They don't care where you went to art school. They don't care how many Emmys you won or how
  many Oscars you won — IT REALLY IS NOT RELEVANT TO THEM.** What they care about is **they have a
  list of problems they want solved, and they're trying to find out IF YOU CAN SOLVE THEM.**"* — with
  the qualification: *"I'm not suggesting you don't bring your credentials up **in the discussion of
  the solution** — but you have to talk about the problems they have."*
- *"**Nobody really cares about you and what you do**, nobody cares about what computers you use or
  what software."*
- **Where the work is:** company websites needing continuous video; **internal sales-motivation
  video**; trade-show video; annual all-hands video introducing the CEO. *"**Their need for video —
  which is what most of you do — is ENORMOUS.**"*
- **A warning about positioning against incumbents:** *"**be very careful not to do what I just did,
  which is STEP ON THE TOES of the people they have**… be somebody who is **AIDING AND ASSISTING an
  effort that's already underway.**"*
- **And on prestige work scaring the buyer:** *"**the more you talk about your big-time work, the
  more you're going to FRIGHTEN THEM ABOUT YOUR PRICING** — so you have to address early on that you
  can do good work for a price that they can afford."* → his read of the market, flagged by him as
  opinion: *"**this is just my opinion** — the same amount of money is being spent, **it's just a
  VOLUME BUSINESS now.**"*
