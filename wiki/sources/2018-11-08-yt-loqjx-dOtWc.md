## ★★★★ Chris-attributed material

### ⏳ ★★★★★ The filter, dated 2018-11-08

> ★★★★★ *"I'll share something with you that **my therapist shared with me**… **the first thing is: when
> somebody says something, you have to ask yourself — WAS A REQUEST MADE OF ME?** So if **my son** walks in
> the room [and says] *I'm hungry*, **I just sit there and do what I do.**"*

✅ **Same mechanism as [[2020-03-07-yt-dQNPikq2xHo]]** — *"if no request was made, you just move on"* — but
**this telling is from November 2018**, making it the **earliest** attestation in the corpus. The 2020
version should no longer be treated as the origin.

### ⚠️ ★★★★★ But the FRAMING is different, and the difference matters

> ★★★★★ *"**my wife is busy being the caretaker for the family**, and sometimes she can feel like **taken
> advantage of — and rightfully so.**"* → *"[when my son says he's hungry] I just sit there and do what I
> do. **My wife springs into [action].**"*

> ⚠️ **CONTRADICTION / DEVELOPMENT — flagged, not resolved.**
> **2018 (here):** the caretaker is **his wife**; he is the one who *"just sits there"*, and the filter is
> offered as something that would protect **her**.
> **2020 ([[2020-03-07-yt-dQNPikq2xHo]]):** *"my therapist… **identified I'M a caretaker**… I'm a middle
> child, so I'm a caretaker,"* and the filter is what he uses on **himself**.
>
> ⚠️ **Both are his own accounts, 16 months apart, and they assign the role differently.** Plausible
> readings: the therapy work moved from the household to him; he recognised the pattern in himself later;
> or he is describing two different people's over-functioning with one tool. **The corpus cannot
> distinguish these.** ⚠️ **Do not smooth this into a single story** — the 2020 caretaker diagnosis in
> [[../../persona/biography|biography]] §252 is well documented, and **this earlier framing must not be
> used to undercut it, nor erased to protect it.**

📌 **Recorded because the sequence is informative regardless of which reading is right**: the *tool* is
constant from 2018; **who it is aimed at is not.**
