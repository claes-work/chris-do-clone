## ⚠️ Guest material — context only, NOT persona data

Elay Cohen's system, recorded in shape only: goals at day/week/month/quarter/year scale, sometimes
split into **AM and PM goals**; *"**I have become better at saying no**"*, always with an explanation
and an offer; a quarterly **"V2MOM"** exercise (vision, values, methods, obstacles, measures) with his
leadership team; **thinking a problem through on a run, then writing it out**; walking to transit to
extend thinking or call time; and a late-evening execution block after his children are asleep.

⚠️ **His household and family details are his and are not recorded.**
