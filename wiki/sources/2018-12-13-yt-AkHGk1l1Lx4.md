## ★★★★ Chris-attributed material

### ★★★★ The matrix — four bands by job size

> ★★★★ *"I kind of **created this matrix of different size jobs**: **under $5,000 · $5 to $30k · $30 to
> $200k · and over $200k.**"*

⚠️ `5 to 30|30 to 200` returns **0**. The corpus holds many individual price points; **it does not hold
this as a deliberate segmentation he built.**

### ★★★★★ ⭐ The coach's correction, and the threshold that carries it

> ★★★★★ *"What I realized when I was working with **my business coach** many years ago is this: **I thought
> I was playing at the A LEAGUE, the highest level** — but **my coach told me: once you get into the north
> of $200,000, most clients can CHOOSE TO WORK WITH WHOMEVER THEY WANT.**"*

> ★★★★★ *"That means the level of competition **isn't just who's best in your town, who's best in your city
> or state — it's WHO'S BEST IN THE WORLD.**"*

✅ **The threshold is the usable part**: at roughly **$200k+**, geography stops protecting you. Below it
you compete locally; above it the client's option set is global. **That is a concrete, falsifiable claim
about the shape of the market**, not a motivational line.

⚠️ **Note the shape of the intervention** — his coach corrects **an inflated self-assessment**
(*"I thought I was playing at the A League"*). **This is the third such correction from the same coach in
the corpus**, after *"you're hiding in your room"* and *"you're not doing a great job"*
([[2019-11-09-yt-7snev8P4ENU]]).

📌 **The coach is unnamed here**, but [[../entities/kier-mclaren|Kier McLaren]] is the documented
*"long-running business consultant/coach (~10–15 years)"* and the phrasing matches the other two. ⚠️
**Recorded as "his business coach" — not asserted as McLaren**, since the source does not name him.
