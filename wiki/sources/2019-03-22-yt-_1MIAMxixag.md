# Graphic Design Tutorial: Typography Design & Art Direction pt. 1 (2019-03-22)

> ✅ **Attribution: EXPLICIT SELF-IDENTIFICATION** — *"Hey everybody, **Chris** here."* The strongest
> basis in this repo, and rare on @TheFuturAcademy, which is otherwise instructor-led.

## Summary

★★★★ **A live art-direction session, and — incidentally — the first recorded TYPEFACE PREFERENCE in
the corpus.** He directs a designer (Min) on an editorial spread for an internal newspaper project
(working masthead: *Thirst*).

## ★★★★★ Chris-attributed material

### ★★★★★ ⭐ A stated typeface preference — closing a documented gap

> ★★★★★ *"You're using **Futura** right there. **I like Futura.** **It's part of the identity we've
> been using.** It's really nice."*

⭐ **This resolves part of the gap filed in [[../gaps|gaps.md]] on 2026-07-28.** That entry recorded
that the clone **could not name a single typeface he likes**, and that the five *Fonts You NEED To
Know* clips could not supply one because their *"X is one of my favorite typefaces"* opening is a
**series template with no identified narrator**.

✅ **This source is different in exactly the way that matters**: it is **self-identified**, it is
**unscripted** (an aside inside a critique, not a title card), and the preference is **tied to a
concrete decision** — Futura is *"part of the identity we've been using."* **One offhand line in a
tutorial is worth more than five templated openings.**

### ★★★★ Art direction defined as NOT taking the mouse

> ★★★★ *"How do you give art direction to designers… in a way that **empowers them to make the
> changes that you want**? How do you do it in a way that **doesn't mean that you're sitting on the
> box moving the mouse around yourself**? Because the important part of art direction is to **give
> people feedback so that they can grow as designers** — and you can get the work done and focus on
> other things."*

✅ **Two goals stated as one move**: the designer grows *and* he is freed. This is the same
delegation logic as his **"I do / we do / you do"** ladder ([[../../persona/biography|biography]],
~2000s Blind), applied at the level of a single critique.

### ★★★ The critique in practice

★★★ Praise is specific before the objection lands: *"very clean layout… good use of a **bold
headline, subhead, and body copy, and a byline**."* Then the reservation, and it is about
**hierarchy, not taste**: *"**what I don't love right now is the way that Thirst is in there** —
it's kind of at an angle and it's not quite working."*
