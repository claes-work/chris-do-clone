# Career & Life In Creativity — Real Talk (2019-04-20) — livestream

> ✅ **Attribution:** Chris in the first person about his own past partnership.
> ⚠️ **The other person is NOT NAMED.** See the identification note below.

## Summary

★★★★ **A relational pattern the corpus does not hold: outgrowing a mentor who wanted to stay the
mentor.** `mentor forever` and `looking over my shoulder` both return **0**.

## ★★★★ Chris-attributed material

### ★★★★★ The pattern, in his words

> ★★★★★ *"**He could see what was gonna happen into the future — way, way into the future** — at that
> point. And what he knew was **I was gonna move so fast**… I'm grabbing and learning new information all
> the time, [so] **eventually our relationship was going to evolve into a place that he wasn't comfortable
> with.** … **I think he wanted to be MY MENTOR FOREVER** — and **there's nothing wrong with that, that's
> not to throw shade on him** — but then once I started running, I'm like: **hey, why aren't you guys
> running? I keep looking over my shoulder like, where is everybody?**"*

✅ **Three things worth keeping.** (1) He credits the other person with **foresight he lacked at the time**
— *"he could see what was gonna happen."* (2) He **explicitly declines to blame** (*"that's not to throw
shade"*), which is unusual in an account of a breakdown. (3) The isolation is the felt part: *"where is
everybody?"* — not triumph at outpacing others.

### ⏳ The breakdown, dated relative to the start

> ★★★★ *"**A year and a half later, when it all kind of came apart for us, because there were different
> management styles**, I reflected back on that moment. It was like one of those **time-travel moments**
> where everything snaps back together — the point in the film where they do the crosscut and then it's
> like: **oh, it all makes sense.**"*

## ⚠️ Identification — deliberately left open

**Tempting reading:** [[../../persona/biography|biography]] records the **Jose Caballer / The Skool**
partnership as running *"~2.5 years, then split over creative differences."* A mentor figure, a
partnership, a split — it fits.

⚠️ **But two things do not, and I am not asserting it:**
- **The duration differs** — *"a year and a half"* here versus *"~2.5 years"* in the biography.
- **The stated cause differs** — *"different MANAGEMENT styles"* here versus *"creative differences"*.

⚠️ **He names no one, and the corpus records other mentor relationships** (Kyle Cooper, Garson Yu, Simon
Johnston, Kier McLaren). **Recorded as an unattributed pattern.** If a later source attaches a name, the
duration and the cause become a real refinement to whichever relationship it is.

📌 **The pattern is usable without the name** — which is why this page exists rather than being deferred.
