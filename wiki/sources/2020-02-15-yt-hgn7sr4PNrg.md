## ★★★★ Chris-attributed material

### ★★★★ He seeks the challenge, and names the reason

> ★★★★ *"I do [this] **to be intellectually stimulated** — because **I want to be critical about my own
> thinking.**"*

★★★ The framing he attaches: *"**I seek personal growth and development, so if I don't feel like I'm
growing then I'm dying.**"*

### ★★★★★ ⭐ How he concedes — formally, and gladly

> ★★★★★ *"Every once in a while **my coach used to do this to me — my therapist did this to me all the
> time.** She would ask me a couple of questions and I'm like: ***I submit, you have the superior
> argument, ma'am.*** … **My worldview has changed forever moving forward.**"*

✅ **Three things worth keeping in that sentence.** (1) The concession is **explicit and ungrudging** —
*"I submit"*, not a grudging retreat. (2) It is produced **by questions, not by counter-assertion** —
the same Socratic mechanism he uses on clients, run on him. (3) He reports the outcome as **permanent**:
*"my worldview has changed forever."*

⚠️ **Note who did it to him: his coach and his therapist.** Together with the caretaker filter at
[[2020-03-07-yt-dQNPikq2xHo]], **this is the second stream in which a documented professional habit is
traced back to therapy.** Recorded as a pattern worth watching, not yet a claim.

### ★★★★ The boundary: sparring partner vs. troll

> ★★★★ *"When you compare spending time **intellectually sparring with a troll** — and **that was
> someone who is NOT a troll** — I just **get more out of that.** [That] is where I'd divert the time."*

✅ **The distinction is about return, not about hurt feelings.** He is not avoiding trolls because they
are unpleasant; he is allocating attention to interlocutors who can actually change his mind. ⚠️ `troll`
already returns **39** — the anti-troll stance is held. **What is new is the criterion**: engage where
your view could move.
