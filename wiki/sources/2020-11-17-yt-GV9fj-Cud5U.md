# Zoom Interview Tips & Role Play w/ Petrula Vrontikis (2020-11-17)

> **Attribution:** a two-hander with **Petrula Vrontikis**, a design educator. ⚠️ **The technical
> interview advice is HERS** — camera height, lighting, voice, preparation, the custom deck — and is
> fenced below. **Chris's contribution is the employer's side**: he plays the interviewer in a
> roleplay, then debriefs what worked and why. Only his material is persona data.

> ⏳ **November 2020 — remote-interview era.** ✅ It is also the third time in this run that the
> **magic-question machinery from [[2016-02-13-yt-pKMfPGAZHYI|2016-02-13]] shows up in practice.**

## Summary

★★★★ **The corpus's only source where he is on the HIRING side of an interview at length**, and the
debrief is the valuable part: he names the *"invisible check boxes"* a candidate hit, discloses that
he **didn't read the résumé**, and concedes his own limits as an interviewer. It also yields one
biographical fact.

## ★★★★★ Chris-attributed material

### ★★★★★ Him as the employer — the roleplay

Playing the interviewer to Petrula's candidate:

- ★★★ *"First of all — **I love your energy.** And maybe there's an opportunity here. **You seem like
  Futur material to me.**"* → *"Wow, that sounds fantastic. **I'm intrigued — tell me more.**"* →
  *"**When can you start?**"*
- ★★★★★ **The disclosure that matters:** *"**I'm going to be honest with you: truly, I DIDN'T LOOK AT
  YOUR RESUME. YOU COME HIGHLY RECOMMENDED, and I'm impressed with what I've heard so far.**"*

  ✅ **Corroborates the claim he makes ten months later** at
  [[2017-09-27-yt-UHrjn11F6hc|2017-09-27]]… in fact *earlier*: *"if I agree to have a meeting with
  somebody, it means **I've already checked out the work, OR YOU COME HIGHLY RECOMMENDED from people
  I trust.**"* **The same filter, described from inside the room.**
- ★★★★ **How he proposes to proceed:** *"I think the best way for us to move forward is for me to put
  you on **some kind of TRIAL BASIS with us, where we can see if we're both a good fit for each
  other.**"*

> ⚠️ **The candidate declines to close, and it is instructive** — `[PETRULA]`: *"I think that may be
> where we end up, but I think **it's really important that I actually understand more about what
> your goals are**… I want to make sure that whatever we embark on **has the best chance of
> success.**"* **Hers, not his** — but he lets it stand and praises it in the debrief.

### ★★★★★ The debrief — "the invisible check boxes"

The teaching core of the episode, and all of it his:

1. ★★★★ **Presentation as a message:** *"she looks amazing, she sounds amazing — **and that's because
   there's a lot of time and attention put into this.** It communicates right from the jump: **I'M A
   PROFESSIONAL, PLEASE TAKE ME SERIOUSLY, AND I'M PREPARED.** She's **dressed for the job that she
   wants** — she didn't show up in her pyjamas."*
2. ★★★★★ **Homework, defined as finding gaps:** *"she used a lot of questions and phrasing that says
   to me, as a potential employer: **she's done her homework. She's not just watched the videos — SHE
   FOUND GAPS WHERE WE COULD GROW.** And as somebody who wants to grow my business — **and I don't
   know any business that doesn't want to grow their business** — **she's bringing a breath of fresh
   air and a bag of ideas that really resonate with me BUT I'VE NOT BEEN ABLE TO ACT ON.**"*
3. ★★★★★ **The weave:** *"**then she neatly, very carefully WOVE HER SKILL SET with this idea she's
   talking about into just this PERFECT UNION. And it was just like: I DON'T HAVE TO DO ANY
   GUESSWORK.**"*
4. > ★★★★★ *"**She's hitting all of THE INVISIBLE CHECK BOXES that I have in my mind.**"*

   ✅ **This is the employer's-eye view of the magic question.** At
   [[2016-02-13-yt-pKMfPGAZHYI|2016-02-13]] he told candidates to make the employer *describe the
   profile of the perfect person* so you can read it back. **Here, from the other chair, he names
   what that profile actually is: a set of unstated criteria.**

★★★★ **The realistic close:** *"**if you can do one tenth, one fiftieth of what she's done, you're
giving yourself a really good shot of getting the job — but FOR SURE standing apart from everybody
else who hasn't prepared for this moment.**"* → *"**whatever exercise you have to do — mental,
physical, spiritual — to show up for this and to BE PRESENT, to be connected to this other person,
YOU NEED TO DO THAT, so that you don't blow the interview.**"*

### ★★★★ He pre-empts the obvious objection to his own demo

> ★★★★ *"**I understand our fans and our friends on the internet — the first thing they're going to
> say in the comments is: 'wow, I can't do that. She's super experienced, she runs companies, she
> teaches at ArtCenter — WHO AM I?'** So let's break down what happened."*

★★★ **And he concedes the gap rather than papering over it:** *"**look where you're at and where
Petrula is at — yeah, there's some light between the two of you, I get that.** But what we wanted to
show you is: **if you could have your IDEAL VERSION of your interview, it would look something like
what just transpired.**"*

✅ **Note the phrase *"who am I"*** — the same self-deprecating construction he uses in
[[2018-11-14-yt-ua1jWIzVl1w|2018-11-14]] when naming his sales influences.

### ★★★★ Two honest limits, stated unprompted

- ★★★★ *"Granted, **I'm rusty like a bucket of nails on interviewing, because I DON'T REALLY
  INTERVIEW PEOPLE — there's people who do that for me.**"* ⏳ A dated fact about how The Futur hired
  in 2020, and a limit he puts on his own authority here.
- ★★★ When Petrula points out *"Chris, you can't call yourself a conventional employer or business
  owner"* — *"**that is true. That is all very fair.**"*
- ★★★ On the roleplay itself: *"**you're a candidate who's interviewed in ways that no candidate has
  ever interviewed with me before**… this is like one of those unreal, god-level interview things."*

### ★★★★★ A biographical fact

> ★★★★★ In the outro, recommending her course: *"**she was even MY TEACHER back in 2007.**"*

⚠️ **The context is not given** — no institution, format or subject is named, and by 2007 he had been
running Blind for over a decade. **Record the statement, not an interpretation of it.**

## ⚠️ Petrula's material — context only, NOT persona data

Hers, and the bulk of the practical advice: the first three seconds decide an impression *"before you
even open your mouth"*; **"you're a designer — design your environment"**; camera at eye level to
emulate standing three or four feet apart; strong front lighting *"because most communication is
non-verbal"*; voice projection framed as *"good posture, but for your mouth"*, with a smile audible in
the articulation; **practise your voice "almost like an instrument"** — *"how can your voice instil
trust and confidence and authority?"*; never open with *"can you hear me?"*; and, before the
interview, **ask how long it is and how many people will attend, then research them** — *"**you're
replacing these unknowns with knowns, and therefore the fear level goes down.**"*

⚠️ **Also hers, and it is a direct caution:** *"you want to be very careful, because **you've got to
maintain humility. I DIDN'T TELL CHRIS I'M GOING TO BE AN ASSET TO YOUR COMPANY — NEVER EVER DO THAT.
Chris has to determine whether or not he needs me.** … I've read it so many times in cover letters:
'I know I will be an asset to your company.' **No you don't. That's awfully presumptuous.**"*

⚠️ **The "don't poke through your website, build a custom deck" advice** is stated in the episode with
an aside — *"because I can do that myself, and it's not the right tool for the job"* — whose speaker
**cannot be reliably assigned** from the captions. **Fenced as uncertain.**
