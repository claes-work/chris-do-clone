## ★★★★ Chris-attributed material

### ★★★★ The frustration, diagnosed

> ★★★★ *"When you hire your first freelancer, **don't get upset at them.** Why can't they just do this
> really fast? Why can't they edit it the way I do it? …You get frustrated with people because you have a
> bias called the **CURSE OF KNOWLEDGE** — the more you know, at a certain point **you forget that there
> was a point in time when you didn't know any of this stuff.**"*

✅ `curse of knowledge` returns **11**, so the bias is held — ⚠️ **but the existing hits are
content-strategy** (teaching/explaining). **Applying it to hiring and to one's own irritation is the new
part.**

### ★★★★★ ⭐ The coach's correction, and the arithmetic

> ★★★★★ *"I had this same problem **until my coach started telling me: Chris, ARE WE BEING REALISTIC RIGHT
> NOW?**"*

✅ `being realistic right now` returns **0**. ⚠️ **Vocative inside reported speech** — his coach addressing
him, quoted by him. ⚠️ **The coach is unnamed here**; the habitual framing fits the documented
[[../entities/kier-mclaren|McLaren]] relationship but **is not attributed on this source.**

> ★★★★★ *"What he also told me is: **if they could do 75% of you** — yes, that's **25% short of you.**
> But **if you had TWO people doing 75% of you** — and I know the math doesn't always work out like this —
> **they're now 1½ times you, and you can take on more work.**"*

✅ `25% short` returns **0**. ⚠️ **The move is to stop comparing one hire against yourself and start
comparing the *sum* against yourself.** ✅ He supplies the fairness argument himself: *"**they are most
definitely not 100% you. And if they were, they would charge 100% of you.** That seems pretty fair."*

### ★★★★★ ⭐ "Coach the gap"

> ★★★★ *"We know that **in that last 25% is where the magic is** — where we obsess over the details, or
> finesse the edit, or make sure the sequence of shots is right, or drop in some sound design."*

> ★★★★★ *"So this is what we need to do. **COACH THE GAP.** The part that you focus on is **the part
> that's missing** — this 25% — and **we want to help them get there.**"*

✅ `coach the gap` returns **0** corpus-wide. ⚠️ **This completes the model rather than repeating it**: the
75% arithmetic says hire anyway; *coach the gap* says **where your attention goes afterwards** — not to
redoing the 75%, but to closing the 25%. `where the magic is` returns **3**, none in this construction.

⚠️ **He names the client-side risk honestly**: presenting 75% work *"they may feel a little weird, it's a
little off, they can't quite put their finger on it — maybe it's so obvious that they can."*

### ★★★ The reason not to stall

> ★★★ *"Some freelancers who get a lot of work don't want to grow… they think **the work will always
> come. Until one day it doesn't** — and then they look back and say: **when the iron was hot, I should
> have struck it.**"*

⚠️ Recorded doctrine in a new phrasing — **voice datum, not a new belief.**
