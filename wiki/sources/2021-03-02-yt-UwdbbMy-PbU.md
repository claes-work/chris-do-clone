## ⚠️ Guest material — context only, NOT persona data

Recorded in shape only, and **explicitly dated**: her five essentials cover engaging before you post,
**contributing** rather than broadcasting, **creating native content** (keeping media on-platform so
the platform does not suppress reach), researching the groups and hashtags a target person lives in
and **showing up there repeatedly** to make a connection feel organic, and connection etiquette —
stating your reason when you request, and accepting requests afterwards.

⚠️ **Her explanation of platform incentives** (*"LinkedIn doesn't want you to send traffic away… they
want to put their ads in front of new eyes"*) is her characterisation, not a verified fact, and is
**2021-specific.**
