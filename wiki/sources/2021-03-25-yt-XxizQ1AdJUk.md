## ★★★★ Chris-attributed material

### ★★★ The never-discount rationale (recorded doctrine, sharper statement)

> ★★★ *"We are **training customers to expect a discount**… when you build a **relationship based on
> price**, when the price goes up the customers find somebody else. **They start to think of you as the
> place to go when they DON'T have enough money.** And so when they DO have enough money, they go and hire
> somebody else who doesn't discount."*

✅ The anti-discount position is well covered. ⚠️ **Voice datum, not a new belief** (settled rule) — but
the *"the place to go when they don't have enough money"* formulation is unusually crisp.

### ★★★★ Five named exceptions — he breaks his own rule deliberately

> ★★★★ *"**Every rule is made to be broken.** So let's talk about what the exceptions are."*

| # | Exception | Mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Best/most loyal customers** | years of history, first shot at good projects, pay on time |
| 2 | **The "Costco model"** | bulk: *"I can give you a discount if you book three projects RIGHT NOW at $240K"* — economies of scale, no team turnover |
| 3 | **Concept viability** | Kickstarter-style early-buyer discount |
| 4 | **Prelaunch** | ⭐ their **own** practice: discount for accepting ~30 days of delivery risk on an unfinished course |
| 5 | **Early payment** | ⭐ **"2% net 15"** |

✅ `Costco model` returns **1**, dated **2024-02-26** — ⚠️ **this predates it by ~4 years.**
⭐ `net 15` returns **0** corpus-wide, and it comes with a **mechanic the corpus does not hold**:

> ★★★★ *"If you're going to bill a project for $100, when you build the estimate, **build it at $102.**
> So you've already made the money… **and if they miss it by one day, you get the $102.**"*

### ★★★★★ ⭐ "The reverse sale" — a NAMED concept at 0 corpus-wide, with a worked case

> ★★★★★ *"I want to introduce you to a concept that most people don't talk about… **It's called THE
> REVERSE SALE.** …Some of you know **I make a typography course**… **It's $149.** So I'm having a
> conversation with **Ben Burns** and I tell him: **Ben, this class is TOO CHEAP.** I believe people
> question whether or not this class is valuable at this price point… **I want to DOUBLE the price.**
> Charge **$299**… **email everybody** that in a certain number of days the price will be going up."*

> ★★★★★ 🗣️ *"And you know what happened? The first day the email went out — **$16,000 in sales in 24
> hours.**"*

✅ `reverse sale` returns **0**. ⚠️ 🗣️ **The $16,000 figure is SELF-REPORTED and unaudited** — recorded as
