### ★★★★★ ⭐⭐ He forged his own school grades, and says why

> ★★★★★ *"I got a **C**, and I knew that coming home with a C — **with my dad, was just going to be the end
> of it.** …**My mom has all these drafting tools**… she had this massive thing, like an **electric
> eraser**. I would sit there and just **erase that C away**… take a really **blunt pencil**, just to get
> it to the right stroke width, and then I would find on the front of the grade a letter B, and I would
> just **carefully trace and change that C to a B using the CARBON PAPER** — smudge a little bit, and kind
> of just disguise it."*

✅ `carbon paper` and `electric eraser` both return **0**. ⚠️ 🗣️ **Self-reported, and told voluntarily** —
recorded factually and without editorialising. ⚠️ **Two things it corroborates**, both already in the
corpus: the **fear of his father's reaction** ([[2024-01-13-ext-vietcetera]]: *"he was worried I would end
up being poor"*, and the mother having to intervene), and the **early facility with drafting tools** that
sits under his craft material. 📌 He notes the era himself — *"this is pre-Photoshop, everybody"*.

### ★★★★ ⭐ The first real wage: $18/hour as an inker, 4–5× minimum

> ★★★★ *"He said: **I'm gonna pay you 18 bucks an hour.** And for context, **minimum wage back then I
> think was $3.50 or $3.75** an hour. So **I'm making four to five times as much as I used to make.**"*

> ★★★ *"**He made me an INKER.** He had all these pencil drawings, and he would say: okay, here's acetate,
> here's a [Rapidograph] pen — **just ink these things.**"*

✅ `18 bucks an hour` returns **0**. ⚠️ **Read alongside the corpus's other early figure** — *"when I was 16
or 17 I was making three dollars and 25 cents an hour"* (`persona/biography`). **These are not in conflict**:
that is a minimum-wage-tier job, this is the skilled one that followed. ⚠️ **The transcript garbles the pen
name** (*"video graph pen"*); **Rapidograph is the obvious reading but is my inference — bracketed, not
asserted.**

### ★★★★ ⭐ Defending his younger brother — a stated rule about family

> ★★★★ *"**My brother, who was only a year younger**, was in a grade below me… somebody had **kicked his

> ★★★★★ *"**I don't get along with my brother — at least back then I didn't get along with him. But he's
