### ★★★★ ⭐ Blind's revenue band — the floor, which the corpus did not have

> ★★★★ *"I think about **four or five years into my business**, I was able to do about **$4 million a year
> on the low end**, and almost **$7 million on the high end.** So we would **hover between four and $7
> million as a service agency.**"*

✅ `4 million a year` returns **0**; `7 million` returns **10** — ⚠️ **the corpus held the peak and not the
range.** 📌 The biography records **2007 as Blind's "Zenith" at almost $7 million**; this reframes that
figure as **the top of a band he sustained**, not a single spike. ⚠️ 🗣️ Self-reported.

### ★★★★ ⭐ Core headcount

> ★★★★ *"I think we're probably **15 or 16 people.** And we would **swell up with a ton of freelancers.**
> So our **core group was relatively small.**"*

✅ `15 or 16 people` returns **0**. ⚠️ **Useful against the revenue figure**: $4–7M through a core of ~15
with a freelance surge layer. ⚠️ 🗣️ Self-reported, undated within the agency era.

### ★★★★ ⭐ The New York agency tour — cost stated in temperament

> ★★★★ *"I'd get in the plane and we **book a car service for multiple days.** And we would **hit agency
> after agency in New York.** For multiple days. **It's exhausting for an introvert.**"*

✅ `exhausting for an introvert` returns **0**. ⚠️ **The corpus records his introversion as a trait**
(`socially awkward` = 20 hits) but not as a **cost he paid to do business development** — that is the
addition. ✅ Consistent with the Vietcetera line ingested the same day: *"my idea of a good time is to stay
home."*

### ★★★★ ⭐ Superfan, not troll

> ★★★★ *"If you approach it from the **superfan** — like, **I love this so much, and I thought it'd be
> neat to have this, this and that**… it's **my love letter to Instagram**… **same work, same initiative,
> hopefully the same outcome, but two totally different approaches.**"*

✅ `as a troll` returns **0**; `superfan` returns **4**, none in this contrast. ⚠️ **The claim is that the
posture changes the reception, not the work** — *"same work, same initiative… two totally different
approaches."* 📌 A usable rule for unsolicited redesigns and public critique.
