## Summary

★★★★ **A three-part pre-call method, and its most valuable part is a SEQUENCING claim the corpus does not
state this way.** His frame: *"**90% of the sales calls that you're on is predetermined by the preparations
that you do**… and it doesn't require negotiation skills or sales hacks or verbal ninjutsu."*

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)

- **[2023-10-24] Pre-qualification is positioning work, not a phone screen.** State on your own site who
  you serve, what you charge and which problems you solve, so prospects **self-select out** before contact.
  With staff, add a human buffer who asks budget, timeline and problem before passing anyone through.
- **[2023-10-24] Budget and timeline come BEFORE scope.** If they cannot afford it or the timeline is
  impossible, scope discussion is wasted — and the right move is to end the call so they can find a
  partner who can help.
- **[2023-10-24] State of mind is a preparation step, not a mood.** An abundance mindset prevents
  neediness; neediness repels. Confidence is sourced from **demonstrated ability and repetitions**, not
  from affirmation.
- **[2023-10-24] Enthusiasm transfers mechanically.** He names **mirroring** as the reason and treats
  emotional state as something the client will catch either way.

## Notable verbatim quotes

### ★★★★ ⭐ The sequencing argument

> ★★★★★ *"**There's no point to talk about scope if they can't afford it and the timeline's impossible.
> You're not a magician — you can't turn straw into gold.** So this is not going to work. You need to then
> get off the phone, **because they deserve to get off the phone and find the right partner who can do
> this.**"*

✅ `straw into gold` returns **0**. ⚠️ **Note the framing of ending the call** — as something owed to the
client, not as a rejection. ✅ And the callback to step one: *"if you had followed the first part of what
I said, **you wouldn't even be here in the first place** — because these are unqualified leads."*

### ★★★★ ⭐ Self-selection, stated as a restaurant

> ★★★★ *"It's almost like you created a restaurant and you say **we only serve vegan options** — so people
> don't come in like *why, I notice on the burger there's not enough beef on the patty*. You're like:
> **yeah, because it's a vegan restaurant.**"*

✅ `vegan restaurant` returns **0**. 📌 **Another domestic, concrete image doing analytical work** — the
same voice pattern as the pasta-sauce process analogy.

### ★★★★★ ⭐ What he tells himself before a call

> ★★★★★ *"The mindset I go into it with is: **I know I'm better than anybody that the clients can hire.
> They just have to be smart enough to pay me so that I can share my gift with them.** **That's what I
> tell myself.**"*

✅ `smart enough to pay me` returns **0**. ⚠️ **He explicitly frames it as SELF-TALK, not as a claim about
the market** — *"that's what I tell myself"* — and immediately undercuts it with a joke (*"and gosh darn
it, you're good-looking and people like you"*). ⚠️ **Do not promote it as a stated belief about his own
superiority**; it is a pre-call ritual and he marks it as one.

### ★★★★ ⭐ The testimonial loop

> ★★★★ *"Read the testimonials that clients have given you — and if they recorded audio or video, **cut
> those up and put them on a loop and just listen to them.** You've got to stop all that negative
> self-talk. **Just believe what people already see in you.**"*

✅ `put them on a loop` returns **0**. ⚠️ **A mechanical intervention for a psychological problem** —
the same move as locating creative block in a missing procedure.

### ★★★ Neediness, and why scarcity works

> ★★★★ *"**Nothing is more repelling than someone who's needy**, who's desperate for the work. In fact
> **we want that which we cannot have** — it's why the attractive person is so desirable, why the home
> that's never on the market gets bid up, why certain luxury goods carry a premium: **because they're not
> so easily available.**"*

✅ `nothing is more repelling` returns **0**. ⚠️ **Recorded doctrine (scarcity/desire) in a new
formulation** — voice datum rather than a new belief.

### ★★★ Mirroring — already held, one new application

> ★★★ *"There's a term neurobehavioral psychologists use: **mirroring**… when you smile, when you laugh,
> **the other person smiles inside their mind.** …And when you're around people who are scared, **you're
> on edge yourself.**"* → *"**No one wants to hire someone who's scared, nervous and anxious** — it creates
> anxiety in them. **Are you safe to spend money with?**"*

✅ `mirroring` returns **13** — thoroughly held. ⚠️ **The new part is the inverse**: anxiety mirrors too,
and the client reads it as a **risk signal about you**. ✅ He pre-empts the eye-roll himself: *"I'm not a
woo-woo mystic kind of person."*
