## Summary

★★ **Low persona yield, but a clean example of his interview technique** — including the opening
question he uses to get past modesty, and his habit of **closing open threads before moving on.**

## ★★★ Chris-attributed material

### ★★★★ The opening question — engineered against modesty

> ★★★★ *"There are thousands of people, **tens of thousands, maybe hundreds of thousands** of people
> who have a podcast **who never really make a dent** — they're still stuck in the *I got 100 downloads
> per episode*. **What do you think makes you so unique? What is it about you that people are drawn to
> …? AND YOU DON'T NEED TO BE HUMBLE HERE. Just really, let's call it out. WHAT DO YOU BRING TO THE
> TABLE THAT MAKES YOU YOU — and what's your SECRET SAUCE?**"*

✅ **Note the construction**: he establishes the comparison set first (*most podcasts never make a
dent*), then **explicitly suspends modesty** — *"you don't need to be humble here"* — which is a
reusable move for getting a guest to state their own differentiator.

### ★★★ Thread discipline, stated out loud

> ★★★ *"**More threads are opening up here. I want to CLOSE A COUPLE before I move on to the next
> thread**"*

✅ Consistent with the listening method described at [[2023-02-16-yt-bNgRrcIcaFY|2023-02-16]] — the
eight-page notepad of *"rabbit holes, things I circle, follow up on this."* **Here the bookkeeping is
audible in the conversation.**

★★★ **And the follow-up questions are specific and factual** — which agency, what the research
measured, whether he had a role in a particular casting decision — rather than general. When the
answer is no, he accepts it and moves on without filling the silence.

## ⚠️ Guest material — context only, NOT persona data

Recorded in shape only: twenty years in the marketing and ad-agency business; work across several
named agencies; a period on a large wireless-carrier account where **eight agencies worked the same
client across the country** — *"they pitted us against each other at certain times, they brought us
together at other times; it was an interesting dynamic"* — and category/brand development-index
research on share of voice versus share of market. **All his.** He also says his own trajectory
*"follows a lot of what you talk about with personal branding."*

⚠️ **His employers, his campaigns and his show's ranking claim are his self-report** and are not
verified here.
