## Summary

A three-minute definition of branding, delivered by the person who wrote it.

Neumeier works by subtraction first — a brand is **not** a logo ("a logo is a very useful tool for a
business, but it's not the brand; it's a symbol for the brand"), **not** a product, **not** a promise
("there's some truth in that — it does end up acting as a promise, but that's not what it is either"),
and **not** the sum of impressions ("if you're trying to sell a lot of impressions I can see where
that might be useful to you, but from a business's point of view, why do they want that?").

Then the definition: **a brand is a result — a customer's gut feeling** about a product, service or
company. It lives "in their heads, in their hearts"; customers "take whatever raw materials you throw
at them and they make something out of it." Hence the striking consequence: *"when you create a brand
you're not creating one brand, you're creating millions of brands"* — one per person — which he
equates to **reputation**, necessarily varying between people, and fine so long as it's "mostly where
you want it."

He closes on scope: because the brand is the *result*, almost the whole business is implicated —
products, product design, messaging, look and feel, culture, employee behaviour, even finance (which
green-lights it all). *"Almost everybody in a company is affecting the brand… or hurting the brand."*

## Key claims

_All statements attributed to **Marty Neumeier**, NOT to Chris Do. Dated to publication; retained as
entity/influence context only._

- [2024-01-11] **What branding isn't:** not a logo (a logo is "a symbol for the brand"); not a product
  ("people talk about buying this brand or that brand — they're really talking about buying one product
  or another"); not a promise; not "the sum of all the impressions that a company makes on an audience."
  (paraphrase/quote)
- [2024-01-11] **What a brand is:** *"A brand is a result. It's a customer's gut feeling about a
  product, a service or a company. It ends up in their heads, in their hearts. They take whatever raw
  materials you throw at them and they make something out of it — they're making it, they're creating
  it."* (quote — the canonical Neumeier definition)
- [2024-01-11] **You create millions of brands, not one.** *"However many customers or people in your
  audience, each one has a different brand of you. So a brand is like a reputation… and everyone's
  going to be a little bit different about what that reputation is, and that's okay as long as you've
  got it mostly where you want it and it's beneficial to the company."* (quote/paraphrase)
- [2024-01-11] **The designer's error and the client's error.** From the designer's side: *"it was my
  gut feeling about whether this is going to work or not, and then I would sell it as hard as I could
  to get the client to sign off."* From the client's side: *"it's a checklist — I got the logo, I got
  the tagline, I got the ad campaign, check, check, and they think they're done. **None of that's
  right.**"* (quote)
- [2024-01-11] **If you don't start from the result, you don't know what you're doing.** *"A brand is
  the result of that, and if you don't start there you don't know what you're doing. You think you
  know what you're doing, but you don't."* (quote)
- [2024-01-11] **Scope: branding takes in almost the whole business.** The reputation is created through
  "the products we're putting out, the design of the products, the messaging we're putting out, the look
  and feel of them, our culture, how our employees behave." Even finance is implicated "because finance
  has to green-light all these things." *"Almost everybody in a company is affecting the brand, doing
  something with the brand, doing it for the brand, or hurting the brand. You've got to think of it
  that way."* (paraphrase/quote)
