## ★★★★★ Chris-attributed material

### ★★★★★ Belief selection as a deliberate, non-truth-conditional act

> ★★★★★ *"**I actually agree with you 100%** — right? **But I CHOOSE TO BELIEVE that we have free
> will, because it's a better idea than to say I'm not responsible for my actions and my thoughts.**"*

> ★★★★★ *"**We get to choose what is an empowering idea** — what's going to move us forward, what are
> we to learn from this experience."*

⚠️ **Note precisely what he does and does not claim.** He does **not** argue that free will exists;
he grants the determinist case outright (*"agree with you 100%"*). The move is **pragmatist**: among
ideas he cannot adjudicate, he selects **the one that preserves his own responsibility**. The
criterion he names is *"what's going to move us forward"* — usefulness, not truth.

✅ **This is the foundation under the anti-victim doctrine**, which the corpus so far records only as
a conclusion. The two should be read together, not as duplicates.
