# The Daily Practice That Has Changed My Life & Income (1 Change Most People Ignore)

> **ATTRIBUTION — Chris Do solo, first person throughout.** Fully persona-eligible. Two outside voices are
> quoted and credited: **Leila Hormozi** (on humiliation as the cost of entry) and an unattributed
> proverb (*"calm seas don't make great sailors"*).

## Summary

★★ **The single most complete first-person account of Chris Do's earning arc in the corpus to date.**
Framed as a reply to a comment — *"most designers will never even be in a room with someone that might
offer $50,000 for a design"* — it becomes an autobiographical argument that the belief system precedes the
experience, not the other way around.

He names the one advantage he will concede (*being raised in America*) and rejects the rest: the family
arrived as **refugees in 1975**, not speaking the language; English is not his first language; they moved
from the **lower socioeconomic strata to upper middle class by the time he was 18**. **ArtCenter** was
funded by four things stacked together — a **50% scholarship**, a **Stafford loan**, a **contribution
from his parents**, and **two maxed-out credit cards** — leaving *"quite a bit of debt in student loans
and credit card debt."* No grants.

Then the arc, told as a repeated experiment rather than a plan: an **ArtCenter job-board posting** for
**bank brochures at $30/hour** → freelancing *"not even a year out of school"* at **$300/day**, which was
accepted → then **$400, $450, $500, $650, $700** → *"a theoretical cap"* he refused to accept as final →
today **$30,000 for a 45-minute talk**. His own summing-up: *"I've traveled all the way from $30 an hour
to quite literally $30,000 an hour."*

The mechanism he names is a loop: **belief → what you say → what you do → the result → which reinforces
the belief.** The corollary is that changing what you say or do is downstream; the intervention point is
belief.

Two guards he puts on his own advice, both notable: *"**I'm not here to sell you shortcuts**… you have to
put in your 10,000 hours of deliberate intentional practice"*; and the acknowledgement that another 10,000
hours of *craft* only yields incremental financial improvement — the rest is positioning, communication,
marketing and sales.

The closing third is a taxonomy of the three voices that block the change: **the exceptionalist** (*"it'll
work for everyone except me"*), **the procrastinator**, and **the perfectionist**.

## Key claims (2024-03-12)

- **The fleas-in-a-jar parable** as his model of learned limits: fleas conditioned by a lid stop jumping
  past the jar's height even after the lid comes off.
- **The tuna-sashimi story** as the counter-model: he once nearly vomited trying raw fish he'd ordered by
  accident; sushi is now his favourite food. Evidence that first reactions are not verdicts.
- ★ **The one conceded advantage**: *"the greatest advantage that I have in my life is I was raised in
  America."* Everything else — silver spoon, trust fund — he calls *"totally untrue."*
- **Biography, dated and self-reported**: refugees in **1975**; lower socioeconomic strata → upper middle
  class by 18; ArtCenter on 50% scholarship + Stafford loan + parental contribution + two maxed credit
  cards.
- ★★ **The rate arc**: $30/hr (ArtCenter job board, bank brochures) → $300/day → 400 → 450 → 500 → 650 →
  700 → $30,000 per 45-minute keynote.
- **The method inside the arc**: *"if they said yes to that, what will they say no to?"* — raise on every
  new job or client until you find the ceiling, then ask what you'd have to become to move it.
- ★ **Belief precedes experience.** *"It's that I have this mindset that the experience happens. Without
  the mindset, the experience doesn't happen."*
- **Self-description (the standing intro)**: *"a loud introvert"*; mission to *"teach a billion people how
  to make a living doing what they love"*; previously a motion designer and director on commercials and
  music videos (**Coldplay, Jet, Justin Timberlake; Audi, Microsoft**); used that to build *"a financial
  nest egg"* to fund education; *"**my true identity is an educator**"*; 20+ years in Los Angeles.
- **No shortcuts**: most videos assume you've already done the craft work; 10,000 hours of deliberate
  practice is the entry ticket, not the differentiator.
- **The three blocking voices**: exceptionalist, procrastinator, perfectionist.
- **Surrender the old identity**: *"I'm grateful for all my beliefs and my culture, my customs and my
  habits — but now, in order for me to ascend the next mountain, I have to release myself of some of this
  baggage."*

## Verbatim quotes

- ★★ *"People happily pay me **$30,000 to have me speak for 45 minutes.** I've traveled all the way from
  **$30 an hour to quite literally $30,000 an hour** — but I'm not the same person. I have the same
  curiosity, I have the same willingness to do the hard work."*
- ★★ *"**I didn't let how my life began determine how it's going to end.** Every part of your life there's
  a chapter and it's unwritten, and you get to decide how it's going to play out."*
- ★ *"So the market told me $30 an hour — that's my experience. And **if I left it there, I would have
  just stayed at $30 an hour.** … But it was my belief system, my philosophy, to ask myself: I wonder what
  the limitation is of what someone would pay me."*
- ★ *"**Your belief system determines what you're going to say**, and what you say is more likely going to
  indicate what you're going to do — and when you have the result of what you do, they inform or reinforce
  your belief system."*
- ★ *"You've **designed a beautiful luxurious prison for your mind**, and you live in it."*
- *"You are absolutely right — **because you manifest it into your life.** You say I'm not worth this, so
  therefore you don't associate with people that are in a position to pay you more than $50,000… you don't
  seek out a peer group who thinks like this because you feel like they're fake."*
- ★ *"It has to be such a radical idea it feels like **a slap in the face** — something that challenges
  your perception of reality and the rules of life. This is why so few people are able to make the kind of
  progress that they need."*
- *"I'm not here to sell you shortcuts, I'm not here to tell you about life hacks about how you don't have
  to have any skill. **You have to have skill. You have to put in your 10,000 hours** of deliberate
  intentional practice."*
- *"After you've done that initial work, **the rest of the transformation is going to happen — believe it
  or not — in your mind, and not the craft itself.**"*
- Credited to **Leila Hormozi**: *"the risk of being humiliated or failure is **the cost of entry** for
  the growth that you seek in your life."*
- ★ *"**Calm seas don't make great sailors.**"*
- ★ *"**If you think I'm expensive, wait till you get the bill from someone who doesn't know what they're
  doing.**"*
- *"I don't know what I'm not capable of, and I don't know what I'm capable of. **All I know is just try
  and see what happens** — and that mantra has carried me pretty far."*
- On being told it's not possible: *"I know this idea is so offensive to so many people that they comment
  with such great vitriol saying this is not possible, I'm a scammer, I'm a liar, easy for you to say,
  this is all fake. **I'm not here to prove you otherwise.**"*
- *"You know what I sit there and I smile like — what, pray tell? *It worked, Chris.* I'm like, you don't
  say. **You don't say the thing that I've been telling you to do for the last nine years**, over and over
  in every form that I know how to say it."*
