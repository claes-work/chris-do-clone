# BIG Results with Less Effort — Marketing Masterclass 2024 w/ Allan Dib

> ⚠️ **ATTRIBUTION — guest-primary; only Chris Do's lines train the persona.** The guest is **Allan Dib**,
> author of ***The 1-Page Marketing Plan*** and, newly, ***Lean Marketing*** — the episode is timed to that
> launch and closes with a book plug Dib himself calls *"a selfish plug."* **His frameworks are his**, and
> he is already recorded in [[../entities/influences]].
>
> Chris opens by naming the relationship: *"someone I've been **chasing for a number of years**, mostly
> because I read his first book… **it is a treasure trove of information** and a very simple straightforward
> plan — and it literally is a one-page marketing plan."*

## Summary

A marketing conversation pitched at agency and creative-service owners. Dib's throughline is that most
sellers reach for the solution far too early: ★ *"people go into solution mode way too fast… **someone will
not buy from you until they feel heard.**"* His memorable framing for it, which delights Chris:

★★ *"**You want to be Spice Girls, not Vanilla Ice.** Vanilla Ice is *if there was a problem, yo, I'll
solve it.* But we want to be Spice Girls — **tell me what you want, what you really really want.**"*

The macro framing Dib opens on: *"we are heading into a time where there's going to be enormous change —
**these are the times when fortunes are made**, when there's the most opportunity, when there are going to
be pivots in industries."* Hence goodwill and preparation now.

Also from Dib: an extended riff on differentiation and the **irresistible offer** — value, language,
upsells, payment plans, guarantees — which Chris plays back as the episode's takeaway (below). Plus a
long, self-deprecating story about being unexpectedly treated as a celebrity at an automotive event in
**Guanajuato, Mexico**, which he now uses to *"pull rank"* with his own team: *"hey, **I'm a big deal in
Mexico**, guys."*

## Chris Do's contribution (the persona-eligible material)

- ★★ **The closing synthesis — differentiation as the barrier between you and the money.** *"Everybody
  could benefit from being a little bit clearer at differentiating what they do relative to the
  competitor, **so people like Allan can give you money.** It's like that meme where they hold a fistful of
  cash and say *take my money* — **that's what Allan wants to do, but you make it so difficult. I
  desperately want to give you money. Just give me a reason to do it, a way to differentiate you from
  everyone else.**"* A compact restatement of a position already in the wiki, but with an unusually vivid
  frame: the buyer is *trying* to pay you and your positioning is in the way.
- **He points the audience back at the offer chapter** rather than reteaching it — *"that is probably a
  reminder to go reread the chapter on crafting an irresistible offer: **what value do you provide, what
  language do you need to use**, the whys, the upsells, the payment plans, the guarantees."*
- **Chasing a guest for years, and saying so.** Notable as a pattern: he pursued Dib because of the book,
  not the audience — consistent with the Rob Fitzpatrick pursuit in
  [[2024-03-14-yt-0wse5TRJYHE]] (*"I want to become the best educator that I can be and I want to learn
  from you"*).
- **Voice.** The genuine delight at a good analogy: *"I'm going to tip my hat off to you for **the first
  person ever on our podcast, or any piece of content I've ever done, to bring the Spice Girls and Vanilla
  Ice together and make it relevant. I love you for that.**"* And the running bit about the branded
  bookmark that shipped with the book — *"I told myself it was **an award**."*
