# Sales and Marketing Secrets from Marketing Growth Hacker John Driscoll

> ⚠️ **ATTRIBUTION — guest-primary; only Chris Do's lines train the persona.** The guest is **John
> Driscoll** of **naked development** (styled *"naked."* — with the full stop). **His company story,
> naming decision and sales philosophy are his.** Chris interviews and reflects it back.

## Summary

The episode's spine is a **naming decision and what it cost and earned** — a useful case because the guest
is candid about both sides.

**The name.** The company answers the phone as *"naked"*, not *"naked development"*, and Driscoll collects
the reactions: *"I said, this is John from naked, and she goes: **John from what?** … *You know that's not
good, right?* **And I've had so many of those comments — it makes me smile, it really does, because I'm
just not uptight about it and I really don't care if you like it.**"* His accounting: *"**so many people
come to us because we are edgy. We gained more than we ever lost, for sure.**"* And his defence when the
team resisted early on: *"that's how brands work — **nobody says anything about Virgin Airlines. We all
like that brand.** … It's often, when it's provocative, [that] people are uncomfortable in the beginning."*

The design detail he offers unprompted, knowing the audience: *"since you have creatives on here — **we
even added the period after the *naked* in the logo, because it's about finality. It's about nothing
more. Don't add.** Because people always want to add — **we're always trying to chase perfectionism. It's
not good.**"* Closing with Reid Hoffman: *"if you're not embarrassed by your first version…"*

**On selling**, his position is a hard one and stated flatly: *"**I'm here for your success. I don't care
if you like me** — if you're successful, you're going to like me."* And on the free-consulting question:
*"**if you're giving out free consulting, your opinion's not very valuable.**"*

Also from the cold open, and worth noting as **his** claim: *"your inbound marketing must be very good for
Peter Thiel to find his way into your thing."*

## Chris Do's contribution (the persona-eligible material)

- ★★ **The synthesis that names why the name works** — and it is the best line in the episode, from
  Chris, not the guest: *"you have a sense of humour, you're provocative, you're willing to push things to
  the edge — **but it can be rooted in your faith, and it's a reflection of your philosophy**, which I
  think is pretty cool. Because you're talking about being **transparent, being honest, direct, not
  caring** — and so **that is being naked and unashamed.**"* This is his standard move: find the
  through-line that makes an apparently gimmicky decision principled, and hand it back as language the
  guest can use.
- ★ **He pushes it one step further than the guest had**, testing the logic: *"if you wanted to go even
  bolder, you could just say **I'm John and I am naked** — as a founder, you know, or co-founder."*
- **He credits the courage rather than the cleverness**: *"it says something about you — **willing to go
  for it and push, and get the team to adopt it and say: we're going to commit to this.**"* Consistent
  with his standing position that a strong point of view makes friends *and* enemies
  ([[2024-04-16-yt-VYKA7hqZeZU]]).
