## ★★★★ Chris-attributed material

### ★★★★★ His own theory: we don't like carrying emotional debt

Responding to the guest's *"any chance you could do me a small favour?"* — and going past the
technique to why it works:

> ★★★★★ *"So it's not just anything, **it's a SMALL favour** — and it's **RECIPROCITY**, [because] you
> said thank you, so **they've already primed themselves to repay it.** And **I have this theory about
> modern decent societies: THAT WE DON'T LIKE LIVING WITH DEBT. EMOTIONAL DEBT we don't want to carry
> — so we want to repay, BECAUSE WE DON'T WANT TO BE SEEN AS SOME KIND OF PARASITE OR USER OR
> TAKER.** … It's **not as rudimentary as trading — BUT IT IS A FEELING OF SELF-WORTH.** You don't
> want the balance [running against you]."*

✅ **Flagged as his own** (*"I have this theory"*) and it supplies a **mechanism** for the give-first /
karmic-equity material the corpus records as a practice: people repay **not to settle a ledger but to
protect their self-image.**

### ★★★★★ Happiness as perpetual growth

> ★★★★★ *"People are like: **what is happiness for you? IT'S PERPETUAL GROWTH.** And people don't
> understand that. **They get to a certain place — and I think you said this about best practices —
> you think that once you get there, you could just REST and not keep going. THERE'S NO SUCH
> THING.**"*

★★★ **With the qualifier he offers himself:** *"unless you become [a Beyoncé or an Elvis]"* — i.e. a
reputation large enough to coast on — which the guest then extends. ✅ **He states the rule and
immediately names its exception**, rather than leaving it absolute.

★★★ **The same idea, applied to method:** *"**the journey of 10,000 miles begins with a single step**…
**don't get caught up with the end — just do CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT.** I love the way that you phrased
this: **rewrite your bio every six months.**" ⚠️ *"Rewrite your bio every six months"* is **the
guest's**, praised by Chris.

### ★★★★ On why plain language beats clever language

> ★★★★ *"What I like about this is **it's not fancy language — IT'S COMMON SENSE LANGUAGE.** But
> language we don't often think of and use. And this is why I think **often times COMMON SENSE IS NOT
> SO COMMON** — but once you lay it out, **you now all have a choice**, and you can say to yourself:
> **I can say this. I could do this.** There's **nothing out of my wheelhouse that I could not say** —
> and you can imagine how the conversation's going to go."*

✅ A statement of what makes a script usable: **it must already be inside the speaker's register.**

## ⚠️ Guest material — context only, NOT persona data

- **Magic words** — pre-scripted phrases that reduce friction. The referral opener discussed at
  length: *"**any chance you could do me a small favour?**"*, where **"small" is the lift**.
- **Gift-wrapping the ask:** *"**it's a gift to you, it's a gift to them, it's a gift to the person
  receiving. And why is it a gift? BECAUSE IT'S WELL PACKAGED.** We know gifts are gifts when they're
  well packaged — it's called gift wrapping. **We are gift-wrapping requests and asks so the recipient
  feels VALUED AND APPRECIATED in the way the ask has been made.** And **the reason people struggle to
  ask is because THEY DON'T KNOW HOW TO PACKAGE.**"*
- **Success, via Paul J. Meyer:** *"**the progressive realisation of predetermined, worthwhile and
  achievable goals**"* — with his gloss: *"**success is a FEELING. You feel successful; you're not
  successful**"*, and *"**you get to decide all of those things.**"*
- **Rewrite your bio every six months.** Plus an aside about an early self-published book of quotes,
  *"before people would productise and create courses out of things."*

⚠️ **The referral-timing material and the three concepts named in the intro (magic words · timing ·
packaging the request) are the guest's curriculum**, not Chris's.
