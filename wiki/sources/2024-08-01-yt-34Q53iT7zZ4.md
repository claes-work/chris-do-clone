# Erwin McManus Teaches How To Identify Your Avatar

> ⚠️ **ATTRIBUTION — guest-primary; only Chris Do's lines train the persona.** The guest is **Erwin
> McManus** — author, communicator, and (self-described) iconoclast. **The Seven Frequencies framework and
> its assessment are entirely his**, and he says so: *"we have an assessment that we took two years to
> develop."*

## Summary

McManus's framework: people communicate on one of **seven frequencies**, and your dominant frequency —
plus your *weakest* — shapes everything about how you land with an audience.

★★ **His demonstration is unusually honest, and it is about his own weakness.** **Command** is his
*seventh* — last — frequency, and he illustrates the cost domestically: *"both my kids have come to me and
said: **please just tell us what to do. Don't be Yoda. Don't be Miyagi. I don't need your Zen, Dad — I
just need you to tell me what to do.**"* And his reflexive answer: *"well, let's think about this."* His
own verdict: *"**it makes me angry. I've created such a deficit of command in my home that my children beg
me for command.**"* His daughter's version: *"my dad never even told me not to drink or do drugs or to
sleep around"* — to which his answer is *"I kind of did, by showing you a better way"*, and hers is that
she can't remember him ever saying it. The reason he gives: *"**for me, commanding someone makes them
weaker.** That's not always true. There are moments where I have to become a commander — but because it's
my furthest frequency, **I have to consciously choose it. It never happens naturally for me.**"*

★★ **The avatar lesson — how frequency selects the audience.** He was booked to speak at **Lewis Howes's**
Summit of Greatness and prepared accordingly: *"Summit of *Greatness* — they're going to be visionary,
they're going to be focusing on being great. So I came in with this visionary concept, and I'm going to
blow them away with a big idea for the future."* Then he met the attendees: *"**it's packed with people
who are there for therapeutic purposes. Everyone there had wounds and they were all there for healing.**
And I listened to Lewis and I realised — **Lewis is a healer.** His frequency, what he emits to the world,
is healer frequency. **So the thousands of people who came to this conference are all…**"* — i.e. the
audience is assembled by the host's frequency, not by the event's title.

That is the actual answer to "how do you identify your avatar": ★★ **your frequency has already selected
them.** Read yourself, and you know who is in the room.
