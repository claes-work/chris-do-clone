# How They Built a Big Marketing Conference From Zero! Big Domino Theory

> ⚠️ **ATTRIBUTION — guest-primary; only Chris Do's lines train the persona.** The guests are the founders
> of a marketing conference (mid-thirties, UK/AU-inflected, describing themselves as having started *"in
> our early 20s"*). **The Big Domino theory and the conference build are theirs.** Chris interviews.

## Summary

**Their headline number**: *"**we've sold 1,100 tickets already — within the first week.**"*

★★ **The Big Domino theory**, which is their framework and the reason the episode exists: rather than
taking many small steps and hoping they compound, pick **one goal that is slightly beyond your grasp** and
work backwards from it — *"something that you think is potentially a little bit out of your league… **but
something that, if you put 110% effort into, you can not just knock over — but knock over your dream goals
so much faster. Like you are saving 10 years of your life.**"*

Their conference was the domino; everything else became a means to it: *"there were **lots of small
steps** — we needed to map out what the book would be, we needed to work out how to self-publish, we needed
to work out YouTube — **but they were small steps with this greater short-term goal of knocking over this
big domino faster.**"*

★★ **Their diagnosis of clients, and it is a sharp one**: *"**their dreams aren't big enough in the short
term.** They all have great dreams longer term that they want to get to — but we think **it's going to
take them twice as long to get there with that kind of mentality.**"* And the mechanism they name:
*"taking lots of small steps **also gives you an excuse to procrastinate** on all the things that are
important and impactful — because you believe that **right now you don't need to be doing those big things,
you don't need to be behaving as if you are that person**… **that's what happens in the future.** But why
not now?"*

**The energy they're trying to reproduce**: *"when we were doing this we were in our early 20s, **we had
nothing to lose, we weren't jaded, we were just hungry** — and that kind of youthful, nothing-to-lose
energy is something that… now we're in our mid-30s and **we're still trying to channel early-20s [us] with
every single project.**"*

★ **And a good operator's note for anyone running events**, delivered to camera as advice to peers:
*"**don't capitalise on the hype. If you've done a good job, people will buy.**"*

## Chris Do's contribution (the persona-eligible material)

Chris is the interviewer here and the yield is **thin by volume** — this page is filed accordingly. The
substance is the guests'.

Worth noting as **convergence, not new material**: their *"dreams aren't big enough in the short term"*
lands on the same point as Chris's own **chunking / small-steps-toward-a-goal** material and his
**exit-indicator** framing ([[2024-06-13-yt-Tzb7LmtSF3c]]) — but from the opposite direction. Chris
generally argues for **chunking down** to make a commitment achievable; the Big Domino argues that
chunking can become **an excuse to defer the important thing**. ★ **That is a genuine and useful tension,
and it should be recorded as a tension rather than resolved** — both are true under different conditions
(chunking helps a buyer commit; it hurts an operator who uses it to avoid the big bet).
