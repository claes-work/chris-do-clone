# How Two Women Built a $200K+ Design Summit (From Scratch)

> ⚠️ **ATTRIBUTION — guest-primary; only Chris Do's lines train the persona.** The guests are the two
> founders of an Australian-run virtual **design summit**, started when *"the pandemic hit in 2020 and we
> saw the design community freaking out."* **The business model and all numbers are theirs.**

## Summary

A candid, numbers-open account of a **free-ticket → paid-upgrade virtual summit**, which is why it earns a
page despite being guest-primary: the economics are stated plainly and are unusually well documented.

**Their model** (theirs, fenced): a **free ticket** to the summit, with a **30-minute window** after
signup to buy the bundle at the lowest price — *"the bundle price of like **$88** is what people get
offered for 30 minutes once they get their free ticket, so that's the cheapest you're going to get it"* —
after which *"the price varies as the summit goes on… **it maxes out at just over $200 Australian
dollars.**"* Plus order bumps: *"previous summit replay packs and stuff."*

**Last year's numbers** (theirs): **12,500 free ticket holders**, **2,186 orders**, **23 speakers**, and
**gross sales over A$213,000.** Their goal for the coming year: the same free-ticket volume with a **50%
conversion**.

★ **The affiliate mechanism is the interesting part**: *"**all speakers can get affiliate cash** for all
the people they send to the summit — **so there's an incentive for speakers to be part of it, because
they're giving all of this away for free. It's really nice of us to be able to pay them.**"* Their biggest
affiliate earned **A$13,000** — and it was **James Barnard**, who *"I think did **one Instagram reel**"*
(cf. his own episode, [[2024-06-16-yt-HTTW0A3kxxA]]). Their affectionate verdict: *"he's very good at it…
**I hate him.**"* They also note he *"has been a speaker with us since the beginning… **since we were not
making big dollars and we didn't have a huge audience** — and it's been awesome that he stuck with us
through all of that."*

**Chris's contribution** is the interviewer's: he holds them to specifics (*"let's talk numbers here"*)
before letting them describe the model, and closes on the point below.

## Chris Do's contribution (the persona-eligible material)

- ★ **The survivorship caveat, stated out loud** — and it is the reason this page is worth anything to the
  clone: *"I want to commend you for a couple of different things. One is **your ability to mobilise and
  get something up and running** — and **we wouldn't be talking today if the first one was a failure.**"*
  A small, honest acknowledgement that the interview format selects for success stories. Consistent with
  his standing scepticism about founder narratives.
- **Method note**: he asks for **target** numbers before **historical** numbers, which forces a stated
  expectation before the flattering result. Worth recording as an interviewing pattern.

## Fenced — the summit founders (guests; do NOT train the persona)

_Attributed to the guests. Context only._

- **Origin**: launched into the 2020 pandemic when *"we saw the design community freaking out."*
- **Reach**: *"last time we had **130 countries**."*
- **Model**: free ticket → 30-minute lowest-price window (A$88) → escalating price to ~A$200 → order
  bumps of previous replay packs.
- **Scale**: 12,500 free tickets, 2,186 orders, 23 speakers, **A$213,000+ gross**.
- **Speaker incentive**: affiliate commission, since speakers contribute free content. Top affiliate
  A$13,000 from roughly one Instagram reel.
- **Loyalty**: speakers who joined before there was an audience.
