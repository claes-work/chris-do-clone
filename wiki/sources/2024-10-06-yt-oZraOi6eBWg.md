# The Future of Branding — Where Design Is Headed Next w/ Brian Collins

> ⚠️ **ATTRIBUTION — guest-primary; only Chris Do's lines train the persona.** The guest is **Brian
> Collins**, co-founder and CCO of **COLLINS** — already an **influence** entity in this repo
> ([[../entities/brian-collins]], recorded for *"poetry as strategy"*). **His career, his firm's strategy
> and his community model are his.**

## Summary

A warm, mutually deferential conversation — *"I just want to **pull up my chair and listen at the feet of
the master**"* / *"oh, I want the feet of **the other master**, Chris"* — that turns into a substantive
account of how a design firm restructures itself when the industry shifts under it.

**Collins's career, as he lays it out** (his, fenced): ~10 years as an executive at **Ogilvy & Mather**;
before that **Foote, Cone & Belding** San Francisco on **Levi's**; and before that the design division of
what he calls the **Duffy Design Group**. Brands: *"Giorgio Armani to Porsche to Levi's to amazon.com to
Disney"*, and at Ogilvy *"Ford Motor Company, IBM, Jaguar, Starwood Hotels — and certainly **the Dove
Campaign for Real Beauty** that my team worked on."* Then **COLLINS**, now *"going into [its] 17th year"*,
co-founded with **Leland Maschmeyer**.

★★ **The strategic turn — a firm deciding to blow itself up.** *"Those are the things we've been working on
over the last two years, and… **we better blow everything up, because we want to become something different
and something new.**"* Two forces named: *"how much of the industry is upside down"*, advertising entering
the brand conversation, and *"**AI has already been here for six years and is now really starting to show
its face.**"*

★★ **And what they built instead: community.** *"What we're trying to figure out is **how do we have access
to a remarkable community — designers, writers, animators, technologists, coders around the world — and how
do we bring a conversation about the future to that community?**"* They run gatherings — one *"in
partnership with **Google**"* with **250 people**, another in San Francisco during the **Figma**
conference. His definition is deliberately unsentimental: *"and I do mean the word community **without…
[irony]** — **people who admire each other, stay in touch with each other, learn from each other, share with
each other, and are in constant conversation with each other.** It turns out **that is extraordinarily
valuable to people right now.**"*

★★ **The two operating principles**, which Chris presses him for and which are the most transferable thing
here: *"**Chris, we're making it up as we go along.** Okay — but there's one thing about it that's true.
**Our principle: there's no strangers. It's just friends you haven't met yet.** That's the first principle.
**And the second principle is everyone who comes in — we're keeping an eye on them**, and if they feel left
out or uncomfortable, my colleagues are so adept, and **they love people.**"*

## Chris Do's contribution (the persona-eligible material)

- ★★ **The question that produces the principles.** He refuses the abstraction and asks for mechanics:
  *"I'm **crazy curious about how these conversations are structured. How do you facilitate this?** I'm
  having a hard time imagining two, 300 people gather — **how do you connect with each other? What do you
  talk about? What is the format? How do you do this?**"* Characteristic: four concrete questions where one
  vague one would do.
- ★ **He tells the audience how to treat the guest in public** — a small, specific act of care worth
  recording as a **voice/relational** pattern: *"Brian has a very distinctive look. **If you ever see this
  man in the street, stop him, say hello — be kind, be respectful of his time** — but just let him know
  where you saw him, **so that we can continue this dialogue** with people just like Brian."*
- **The framing of experience without condescension**: *"you've been in this game a really long time — as
  the kids say, **you're seasoned. You're not old, you're seasoned.**"* Then the actual question: *"I'm
  really curious as to **the patterns that you're seeing and where we need to be**, because **you seem to be
  pretty good at predicting the future.**"*
- **Relationship note, dated**: Collins credits a conversation with Chris **five years earlier** as
  formative, says he has been stopped in the street in **London and New York** by designers who watch the
  channel, and dates their meeting to a design conference in **Cairo**. Team members named: **Ben Kirk**
  and **Tim Goodman**.
