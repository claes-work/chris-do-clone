## What Essam said — HIS material, listed so it can be recognised, not used

**Three principles**, in his stated order of importance:

1. **"Security is an inside job."** Successful entrepreneurs *"very rarely are influenced by external
   circumstances… they create their sense of security and certainty internally."* Analogy: paramedics are
   calm at accident scenes because they must not let *"that primal part of the brain take over."*
2. **Move creative services from "nice to have" to "need to have"** — because in a downturn *"creative
   services are often one of the first things to get cut."*
3. **"Cash is king"** — from an early mentor of his: *"**revenue is vanity, profit is sanity, but cash is
   king.**"*

**His "cycle of scarcity" model** (his term): stimulus → **belief/story** → emotion → response → result.
The intervention point is the belief, made objective by saying it aloud or writing it down, then
challenged — he cites **Byron Katie's four questions**, and his own **court-of-law** exercise (write the
accusation, then ask *"is there anything the defendant might say to that?"*). His case study: a UK
animation studio that lost its biggest client during the pandemic, used the space to raise rates
**over 30%**, and later said *"I actually think that this client dropping out was one of the best things
that ever happened to us."* Closing metaphor: a swimmer caught in a **Bondi Beach** rip tide exhausting
himself swimming for shore when the current would have carried him around to the beach for free.

**His "lightbulb formula"** — a four-step content/positioning sequence, always in this order:
**symptom → assumption → real problem → the how.** Give the best thinking away free, because *"if you just
say the solution is branding, then it feels salesy."* Worked examples: a branding studio whose ideal
client's symptom was *"spending the same on marketing campaigns but not getting the ROI they did 12–18
months ago"*; a web agency that reframed *"we need more engagement"* into a call-to-action and
dedicated-landing-page problem. Supporting line: *"**clarity comes from conversations.**"*

**His four cash tactics**: a **fast-payment discount** (and *"if you're being really cheeky… add a 10 to
15% margin on top of your existing prices"* first), **expiring proposals** (re-quoted higher after
7/10/14 days), **payment gateways** (Stripe, GoCardless) instead of chasing invoices, and **clear payment
terms — paid on deliverables, not on approval.** Framing line: *"**you cannot pay bills with invoices.**"*
He recommends ***Profit First*** by **Mike Michalowicz**, and reports a **weekly Friday cash review with
his financial director.**

**His daily routine**: **"priming"** — a term he credits to **Tony Robbins** — plus consistent
symptom-led outreach and a weekly cash reconciliation.

**Biographical, about him**: he coached *"more than 50 creative business owners through the global
COVID-19 pandemic"*, and *"I ran a creative studio for several years… when I first started that studio it
was around **2012**, and we're just kind of coming out of a recession."*
