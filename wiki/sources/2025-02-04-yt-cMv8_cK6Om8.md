# Why Clients Ghost You & How to Fix It

> **ATTRIBUTION — Chris Do solo, scripted to camera.** Very short (3.8KB). Fully persona-eligible.
>
> The corpus has extensive listening material. **This adds three things it did not have**: a **measurement
> method**, a **diagnosis of ghosting**, and the **introvert framing as the source of the advantage.**

## Summary

★★★ **The introvert framing, which makes the doctrine autobiographical rather than generic**: *"what I've
come to realise — especially coming from the point of view of **an introvert, one who likes to listen more
than speak** — is that ultimately **that became my secret weapon to selling more.**"*

★★★ **The standard he sets for the whole interaction**: *"**if you're doing your job well in the sales
process, it shouldn't feel any different than you catching up with a good friend** to see what's going on in
their business. **There should be no agenda and no ulterior motive.**"*

### ★★★ "You can smell their agenda"

*"Do you notice that when you're talking to someone, **you could smell their agenda**? So **what they think
is a secret agenda — it's a *not so secret* agenda.** When somebody's on stage speaking, **you can smell the
book sell coming.** You can smell the coaching or the mastermind coming. **You can smell all the things
they're trying to get you to think and do.**"*

★★★ **And the cost, which is the argument**: *"how does that make you feel — and **does it take away all the
goodwill that was supposed to be there? You already got us in the room. We're already listening to you.
We're looking up to you** — and **by doing these things you undermine your own goodwill and authority.**"*

### ★★★ The 80/20 rule — with a way to check

*"You want to **engage in dialogue, not monologue.** You want to listen more than you talk. We have a rough
rule — it's an **80/20 rule: you should be listening 80% of the time and only talking 20% of the time.**"*

> ★★★ **The instrument, which is new and testable**: *"do yourself a favour — **after your next sales
> conversation, look at the transcript and analyse for how much you were talking versus the client. If it's
> not anywhere near the 20/80, you're doing something wrong.**"*

★★ **Why it works**: *"by listening to people **they feel heard, they feel seen, and most importantly they
feel understood — and that's the beginning of trust.** … **I'm not going to give you money if I don't trust
you, if I don't feel like you understand me or understand the problem.**"*

### ★★★ Evidence-based questions — and the exploded car

*"You want to be a **very intentional listener**, and **ask questions that are evidence-based — based on
what you just heard.**"*

> ★★★ **The example is the whole lesson**: *"if the client says **my car blew up, and then I need a new
> website** — and you just **move right onto the website** — **you didn't acknowledge the fact that their car
> blew up.** And so they're thinking: **okay, he's only hearing half the things I'm saying. He's mostly just
> interested in what helps him with his agenda.**"*

★★ **And the worse version**: asking about *"some other random question — a video marketing effort, a social
media podcast, **anything that you want to do**"* — while they sit there *"scratching their head."*

### ★★★ The ghosting diagnosis — "send me a proposal" is the kiss of death

★★★ *"Most people, especially in America, are quite polite. **They'll go along with you, they'll answer your
questions, they'll entertain this** — but you know what they're really thinking? **They're not listening,
and I can't wait for this conversation to be over.**"*

> ★★★ *"So they'll say something to the effect of: ***send me a proposal.*** **That is the kiss of death.**
> It's like: **I'm done with this conversation. Please go work on something so I can get out of here — and
> I'm just going to ignore it when it comes.**"*

★★ **The fix, and the payoff**: *"if you want to avoid clients ghosting you — and [avoid] all kinds of false
leads or **feigned interest** — **become really interested in them. To be interesting, be interested.** And
if you can do that, **the person's going to feel, if all things are equal, that you are more qualified to do
the project than the next person they're going to talk to.**"*

## Key claims (2025-02-04)

- ★★★ **Agendas are detectable** — *"you can smell the book sell coming"* — and deploying one **spends
  goodwill you had already been given.**
- ★★★ **80/20 listening, verifiable by transcript audit.**
- ★★★ **Questions must be evidence-based** — built from what was just said, not from your own interest.
- ★★★ **"Send me a proposal" is usually a polite exit, not a buying signal.**
- ★★ **Being an introvert is the source of the advantage, not an obstacle to it.**
