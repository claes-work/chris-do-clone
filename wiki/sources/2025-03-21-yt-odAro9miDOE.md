# Why Your YouTube Channel Isn't Growing (According to MrBeast)

> ⚠️ **ATTRIBUTION — GUEST-DOMINANT, and short (10KB).** This is **Colin and Samir** relaying what they
> learned from **MrBeast**, on Chris's show. **The three rules, the metrics, the title rewrites and the
> Jenga framing are theirs** (and, at one remove, MrBeast's) — **context only, NOT persona training data.**
> Chris asks the questions and makes **one substantive contribution**, which is why this page exists.
> See [[../entities/colin-and-samir]].

## ★★★ Chris's contribution — tension architecture, via Michael Bay and Tarantino

Responding to their point that tension should rise across a video but **not** monotonically, he supplies the
analogy from his own field:

> ★★★ *"There's a lot of classic storytelling stuff that Hollywood has known for a long time. **Some people
> are Michael Bay fans, some people are Quentin Tarantino fans — for very different reasons.** I think
> **Michael Bay's style, coming from music videos, is to keep it at a frenetic pace for as long as possible
> — ★★★ and then we become numb**, and they're not dramatic pieces. **Whereas Quentin will build and build
> and build, and you can't handle any more — he'll let you off the hook for a little bit, he'll switch the
> timeline a little bit, and then he'll build and build.** But it's just this one ginormous thing… **and
> then Hitler gets burned in a theatre** — like, whoa. ★★★ **And I feel emotionally drained and satisfied,
> and I experienced all the highs and lows.**"*

✅ **A rare instance of him bringing his motion-design/film background to bear on platform mechanics**, and
it is a genuine addition: the guests describe *disproportionate* rise and fall; **he names the failure mode
on the other side — sustained intensity produces numbness, not engagement.**

★ **And the concession he makes to their expertise**: *"I think **MrBeast does this probably better than
anybody else** — and the numbers seem to [agree]."*

## ★★ How he ran the interview (recurring behaviours)

- ★★ **He asks for a concrete example twice, and lowers the bar when the first ask stalls**: *"do you
  remember the example — **I'd love to give the audience [one]**… **I want to try to make it concrete.**"*
  Then: *"**it doesn't have to be from MrBeast, it could be anything you guys have done.**"*
- ★★ **He stops to make the audience keep up**: *"okay, let's let that sink in… **everybody got that?**"*

## ⚠️ Colin and Samir's material — theirs, fenced

- **The three rules of YouTube** as they boil them down: (1) *"**if they don't click, they don't watch**"* —
  make the **title and thumbnail before the video** (*"most people don't. 99%. **We still do that**, and it
  always bites us"*); (2) *"**respect their time**"* — tracked as **7-second, 30-second, and average view
  duration**; the hook must back up the title **and open a new question**; (3) *"**do you want more stuff
  like this?**"* — the monetisation and suggested-video question. Packaged as their **YouTube Growth
  Playbook**.
- **The curiosity gap**, and their worked title rewrites (*"let's talk about plagiarism on YouTube"* → *"the
  copy-and-paste culture of YouTube"*, generated with ChatGPT; plus critiques of two celebrity channels'
  titles as *"closed"* stories).
- ★ **Jenga storytelling** — *"you know the tower is going to fall, but with every piece the tower gets a
  little less stable… **tension has to rise as time goes on**"* — and the MrBeast refinement that
  **tension should rise and fall at disproportionate levels**, not steadily.
- ⚠️ **Their commentary on named third-party creators is theirs, and is not recorded here as assessment.**
