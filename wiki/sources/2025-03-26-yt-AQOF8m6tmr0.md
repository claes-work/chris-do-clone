# How to Work Together (Husband & Wife CEOs)

> ⚠️ **ATTRIBUTION — GUEST-DOMINANT. The couple are the GUESTS, not Chris's family.**
> **Mike and Cass Lazerow** — married 25 years, co-founders of **golf.com** and **Buddy Media** (*"which
> sold to Salesforce for $745 million"*), with a book, *Shoveling Stuff: A Love Story*, due that June.
> **Their story, their frameworks and their anecdotes are theirs — context only, NOT persona training
> data.**

> material for it.**

> differ on risk**, and **an unusually unflattering admission about the cost of his own idea-generation.**

## ★★★ The risk asymmetry in his own marriage and business

Asked as a question to the guests, but framed entirely as self-disclosure:

> ★★★ *"I want to go down that path a little bit more with you, because **my wife — who's also my partner in
> business and life, and the mother of our children** — **we see things a little bit differently.** …
> ★★★ **I'm okay with the risk. I'm very risk tolerant. And she's very risk avoidant.**"*

★★★ **How he describes his own decision-making inside that**: *"when there's an opportunity — and we'll put
that in air quotes, **cuz they're not opportunities half the time. I'm totally wrong** — **I don't know what
the next move is, but my gut says this is going to work out.** So I'll do business, I'll go to meetings, I'll
do things with people where **it may not work out.**"*

★★★ **And how he describes hers, without disparaging it**: *"**whereas my wife needs to see what the next
five steps are on that chessboard, because she can't see the play.** She goes: **why are we wasting our
time?**"*

★★ **His own hit-rate claim** `[self-reported]`: *"more often than not I would say **like 80% of the time
I'm right about this kind of stuff** — I'll put myself in a risky, quote-unquote, way, **but then they pan
out to be this other opportunity I could not have seen.**"*

> ✅ **This materially extends what the corpus holds.** `persona/biography.md` records that his wife is his
> business partner; **it has not had his own account of the temperamental split**, nor the mechanism
> (*gut-first vs. five-moves-ahead*), nor his estimate of how often the gut is right.

## ★★★ The admission — the cost his ideas impose on other people

Prefacing a question to the guests, he volunteers something unflattering about himself:

> ★★★ *"**This is what I do to my wife all the time — and to my entire team.** ***I got this idea, let's go
> do this thing*** — **and they literally then have to go and clean up the crap afterwards.**"*

★★ And when the guest confirms it is frustrating, he does not soften it: *"**yeah, it sounds like — sure,
it's frustrating.**"* ★★ He also frames an entire question as a proxy: *"I'm going to ask this question **on
behalf of all the partners who have to suffer with partners who do things and then the other person has to
deal with it.** ★★★ **I'm mostly channeling my wife here.**"*

> ✅✅ **This is the operational cost of the *bias toward action* doctrine, admitted by the person who
> teaches it.** The corpus has *"say yes to the things you can't do and then figure it out"*
> ([[2025-02-23-yt-hC2_b-y6zuc]]) with no ledger of who absorbs the consequences. **This supplies it.**
> **Promote the two together; the doctrine is incomplete without this.**

## ★★ How he ran the interview

- ★★ **He names a reaction he saw and asks for it to be explained** rather than moving on: *"I saw a
  reaction from you, Cass. **I need you to explain the reaction, cuz I don't think I'm a mind reader, but I
  saw something. Please expand on that.**"*
- ★★ **He asks for more of a feeling once it has surfaced**: *"is there anything else you want to expand on
  in terms of **the feeling, and how you process that**?"*
- ★★ **He translates for the audience** at the close, including a generational gloss (*"for the Gen Z-ers
  listening, what I think Mike meant to say is **he didn't have the rizz**"*).
- ★★ **He states what he valued in the conversation, specifically**: *"I've enjoyed **the transparency —
  just the real realness** — [and] your depth of experience."*
