# Are You Stuck in the Matrix With Your Personal Brand?

> **ATTRIBUTION — Chris Do answering one question**, put to him by an interviewer whose voice bookends the
> clip. **Very short (2.8KB) and entirely his.** Fully persona-eligible.
> ★★★ **Kept because it is the fullest worked example of a doctrine the corpus already holds** — that
> stories are instructions — **and because it contains an unguarded emotional disclosure.**

## ★★★ The reading

*"**I'm a big consumer and lover of pop culture**, and **The Matrix is probably my top two or three films of
all time — and I'll argue with you to the death. I'll die on that sword.**"*

★★ **The setup**: *"**Neo is an anagram for ONE** — he is literally *the one*, just scrambled."* He goes to
the Oracle, who says *"sorry kid, **you're not the one**"* — and *"**Neo's not disappointed, cuz he doesn't
believe he's the one either.**"* Then ★★★ **the line he says everyone discards**: *"and she goes — ***not in
this lifetime.*** She just drops it right in there, **and the audience is like: nah, it's a throwaway line,
who cares.**"*

★★★ **The turn**: *"towards the end Neo confronts the agents, **he dies** — and **it's Trinity's kiss that
resurrects him.** ★★ **There's a very Jesus Christ parallel here.** … **You can't be the one until you're
resurrected**, because you have to realise something."* And then the change in what's possible: *"**the
bullets he was dodging before, he just stops and picks one up, casually looks at it, throws it on the
floor — and he fights them one-handed. It's not even a problem for him.**"*

## ★★★ The application — and he casts himself

> ★★★ *"**I feel like I'm Morpheus, and I'm looking at all of you** — ★★★ **and you are the one. You are the
> one — but not in this lifetime.** **I don't think you need to die, but you need to be reborn.** ★★★ **You
> have to really dig into who you are and understand that.**"*

★★★ **The close**: *"**that's not a wall. That's not air you're breathing, Neo.** ★★★ **There are no
constraints to what you can do — there are no limitations except for the ones you impose upon yourself.**
★★★ **I think you [are] the one. It's a question of whether you believe it or not.**"*

> the line is spoken **to** Neo. **Recorded as evidence that his film references are usually sound and the
> earlier slip was recall, not carelessness.**
