# Searching for The Futur. (Daily Dose: Vlog 001)

> **ATTRIBUTION — Chris Do on camera, unscripted, walking a property.** A leasing agent and a colleague
> appear briefly and are **context only**. Fully persona-eligible.
> ★★★ **Two reasons this small file matters**: it is **the launch of a new format** (*Daily Dose: Vlog
> 001*), and it **confirms and expands the "two buildings" datum** that appeared in passing eighteen days
> earlier.

## ★★★ What he is trying to build, and why

> ★★★ *"Something that I need to do is **to have physical space to be able to bring a community together, to
> create content, [and] to create a speakers bureau so that people can practice their talks.**"*

★★★ **His criteria, stated plainly**: *"**high ceilings. I like some character. I don't want a cookie-cutter
building.** … I'm also looking for **street visibility, walkability, and if it's going to be noisy
enough.**"*

★★★ **And the operating shape he names for The Futur** — a useful self-description: *"**we don't run like a
traditional office business. It's really a media production at this point.** So the idea would be **to host
events here, to create content, broadcast, live-stream — [and] to run workshops.**"* → *"**so a bigger open
space would be better.**"* ★★ Requirement: *"something around **2,000 ft² corner to corner**"*, roughly
*"three [or] four times more"* contiguous space than the unit he's viewing.

## ★★★ "Two buildings in Santa Monica" — confirmed and expanded

> ★★★ *"**I have two buildings in Santa Monica**, and the way we used that is — **the portion we had was
> 9,000 ft²**, about 2 or 3,000 [contiguous]. **It was just corner to corner, and totally you could see
> everything — it could accommodate 100 people for a workshop.**"* `[self-reported]`

✅ **This confirms and expands the *"he's got two buildings"* aside from [[2025-04-09-yt-bO4qcpHI1Sw]]**,
adding the location (**Santa Monica**), the scale (**9,000 ft²**, ~2–3,000 of it open), and what it was
for (**100-person workshops**). ⚠️ **Tense recorded as he gives it**: *"I HAVE two buildings"* but *"the
portion we HAD"* — **no inference drawn about current tenure.**

★★★ **The build-out he describes wanting**: *"**build a giant LED panel wall so that the display is
dynamic**, and then we'd **put a lighting grid, put chairs, and invite people to come in — bring in notable
authors and speakers and influencers to teach things. And we would make content.**"* ★★ **Utilisation, stated
honestly**: *"**most of the time empty when we're busy** — then maybe like **three or four days a month we'd
be shooting.**"*

## ★★ The verdict, and how he reaches it

He walks the space, finds it chopped up (*"everything's broken up into small rooms — **I need a larger, more
contiguous space**"*), asks the agent directly whether walls can come out (*"I would probably, **with some
reservations**, say yes"*), and decides on the spot:

> ★★ *"**But the problem is there's like 35 different walls and angles.** … ★★★ **It's not the one. Too many
> challenges.**"*

★ **The humour is dry and physical.** On old buildings: *"**nice thing about old buildings is old. Bad thing
about old buildings — they smell like old buildings.** You walk in, you're like: *what's that smell?*
★ **100 years of history.**"* And on why he's wearing a hat: *"**not because he looks cool, guys** — it's
cuz **it's a waterfall here.**"*
