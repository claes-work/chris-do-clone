# How To Tell Better Stories on Social Media (Daily Dose: Vlog 002)

> **ATTRIBUTION — Chris Do in conversation with a colleague** who asks the questions and is **context only**.
> Fully persona-eligible.
> ★★★ **A landmark for two things**: **the *record in HD* metaphor**, which is the best answer in the corpus
> to *"how do I notice more?"*, and ★★★ **a fourth telling of the therapy account in which he explicitly
> corrects a public misperception about himself.**

## ★★★ Two definitions of art, offered as harmonious

> ★★★ *"The most inclusive, expansive definition of art — there's two that I really love, and I think
> they're congruous. **Number one: art is when I have a feeling and I make something, and when you see it
> and experience it and you have some similar feeling — that's art.** And the other version: **it's an
> invitation to look at the world from a different point of view.** **If you put those two together, then
> you understand what art is.**"*

★★ **And the consequence for the audience**: *"**art doesn't have to be paint on canvas.** It doesn't have to
be charcoal on paper or something carved out of stone. **You can just have a feeling — and I want you to
feel that feeling.**"* → *"when somebody comments *it feels like you just looked right into my soul*…
★★★ **then you know you've done that job** — **if that was the intention.**"*

## ★★★ Why most posts fail — the switch-flipping diagnosis

★★★ *"The reason most of us aren't very good at storytelling in our posts is because **we don't understand
anything. We're just grabbing things and flipping switches up and down** — kind of like **how a layperson
would look at a complicated piece of electronics** that's not working and **just bang on it and flip
things**, and hopefully sometimes it works."*

> ★★★ **The alternative**: *"be much more intentional and be thinking about: ★★★ **why do I feel this way?**
> **And if I can decode that, perhaps another human being would feel the same way** — **so if I pull those
> same levers and push the buttons and the gears, perhaps they have the same emotional reaction that I
> do.**"*

## ★★★ RECORD IN HD — the central metaphor

Asked to explain an instruction he'd given the colleague (*"you told me to start looking at things in HD"*):

★★ **The setup**: *"we aspire, when recording content, **to record in the highest fidelity format — the
highest bit depth, the largest, most uncompressed file possible.** When we went from standard definition to
HD it's **four times the resolution**… **the colours and the imagery look so lifelike.**"*

> ★★★ **The turn**: *"**if you were to experience life recording things on a low-fidelity instrument** —
> **like a Super 8 camera or Hi8 tape** — you're recording **in very low colour bit depth, very low
> resolution, on a format that's not very durable.** So **if you think of your life as you experiencing
> things, recording it through a pinhole camera with a limited set of colours and storage space** — ★★★
> **what do you expect to be able to extract from your experiences?**"*
>
> ★★★ *"**So we want to live life recording in HD** — in the highest definition possible — **so that we can
> recall on these moments and extract pieces of information, or retell that story in vivid detail.**"*

★★★ **And the precision that makes it usable**: *"**when I say vivid detail, I don't mean factually accurate
— but sensory-accurate**, so that **you can pull on those emotions and get people to laugh or cry or feel
angry — because you know what it was that got YOU to laugh, to cry and to be angry.**"*

★★★ **The same idea stated as a habit**: *"you and I could open the same sales email and **both take the
same action** — but ★★★ **I'm going to be sitting there thinking: why am I taking action? How was this
presented to me? What was the headline that got me involved? What was the visual with the headline that got
me hooked?** — **versus just *I'm going to take action.*** ★★ **Notice things so that you can call upon them
in your life and your language and your communication.**"*

## ★★★ "I notice things" — Seth Godin

> ★★★ *"Many years ago I interviewed **Seth Godin** and I said: *Seth, what do you do for a living?* And he
> said: ★★★ ***I notice things.*** **I thought that was really beautiful — and it was true, and it was
> poignant, and it was simple.** **He notices why we behave certain ways**, or what works in your inbox."*

✅ **A concrete recovery from the Seth Godin interview the corpus has carried as debt** — and it is the seed
of the whole *record in HD* argument.

## ★★★ Wisdom is experience plus reflection — and the two ways people fail it

★★ **Credited to Jim Rohn**: *"**Wisdom is experience plus reflection.**"* — *"if you're really young, you
have no experience… and **if you're old and you've experienced lots of things but you don't reflect on
them, then you have no wisdom at all.**"*

★★★ **Failure one — limiting the experience half**: *"we limit ourselves as to **where we'll live, the
languages we'll speak, the foods we'll try, the clothes we'll put on, the kinds of people we'll meet.**
★★★ **We really put a big container on that: *that's not for me.*** … *that book's stupid, I won't like that
food, that's going to look dumb on me* — **and we say all those things because we're clamping down on the
possible experiences.**"*

★★★ **Failure two — skipping the reflection half**: *"we can experience lots of things and be super
open-minded — **but if we don't reflect and think *why is this important to me? Why did I like this? Why
don't I like it?*** — and **go through those things in a systematic way and process it with words** — ★★★
**and be very specific with the words that you use** — **then that reflection has [no] greater meaning.**"*

★★ **The lens he claims for himself**: *"you and I will watch a TV show together [and] **have totally
different experiences, because I'm not watching it the same way** — **I've tuned my brain to watch with a
very particular lens so I can extract the maximum amount of words and experiences, so I can call upon them
again.**"*

## ★★★ The therapy account, fourth telling — and he corrects the record himself

The colleague credits his insight partly to *"years of going to see a therapist."* **He interrupts the
premise:**

> ★★★ *"**I hate to break it to you — I've only seen my therapist LESS THAN 10 TIMES.** ★★★ **A lot of
> people think I've gone to therapy for years, or that I'm still going.**"*

★★★ **His explanation, consistent with every prior telling**: *"**every session was a lesson that I learned
as dutifully as a student as I could** — **I didn't need it two times.** When my therapist said *hey, stop
doing that; ask your wife this question; talk to your children this way and use this exact phraseology* —
★★★ **I did exactly that, so I didn't have that problem again.**"*

✅ **Fourth attestation, and the count holds**: *"about ten"* (10 twice, 11 once, *"less than 10"* here).
✅ **And it corroborates the *children* branch of the two-reasons account** — the instructions he cites are
about **his wife and his children.** ⚠️ **The employees branch is neither repeated nor contradicted. Both
still stand.**

★★★ **And the frustration it leads into, which is recurring**: *"that's the thing that really confuses me
about many people — people in our circle, people I mentor — **they just don't seem to follow directions
really well.** It could be really innocuous things like fashion, or **much more profound: this is what you
need to do in your business and your relationships.** ★★★ **They just don't do it. And I just don't
understand that part.**"*

## ★★ Perfect practice — and the closing line

★★ *"There's something about *practice makes perfect*, but people say ★★★ ***perfect practice makes
perfect.*** You could practice — **but if the way that you practice isn't good, you're just repeating bad
behaviour, bad patterns.**"*

> ★★★ **The close, and it is the best line in the source**: *"**I think we all can speak like this, but we
> have to put in intentional, perfect practice**… it's going to take some time, **but you can get there.
> All of you can — ★★★ because I never thought I'd be here.**"*
