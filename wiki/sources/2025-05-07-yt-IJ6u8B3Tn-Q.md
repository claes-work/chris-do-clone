## ⚠️ Guest material — HIS, and must never train the persona

- **The avoidance mechanism:** *"**the avoidance of any emotion** — there's something weird in our
  psychology in that **anytime we RESIST an emotion, we unintentionally AMPLIFY that emotion.**
  Anybody who suffers from anxiety has had the experience…"*
- **Worthwhile suffering:** *"I don't want to call it *enjoyable* suffering, because suffering is not
  enjoyable — but I guess I'll call it **WORTHWHILE suffering.**"*
- **The self-test, and it is his best-known formulation:** *"**look for that thing that most people
  think is really hard, but for whatever reason YOU KIND OF DON'T MIND IT.** What's the [—] **sandwich
  you don't mind eating?** Because that's probably your secret…"*
- **His own case:** *"**there is a certain suffering in writing that I am PREDISPOSED to** — rereading
  a paragraph five or six times and rewriting it and polishing it over and over. **That is agonising
  for most [people].**"*
- **On the obvious answer being invisible:** *"**it was right here in front of my nose for five years
  and I was looking over here.**"*
