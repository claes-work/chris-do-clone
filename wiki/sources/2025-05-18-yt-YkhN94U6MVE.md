# Business Books for Entrepreneurs (short, 2025-05-18)

> **Attribution:** ✅ Chris. ⚠️ **A second PROVENANCE reading list** — see also
> [[2026-03-07-yt-A-LQwz40G7Y|the marketing three]]. All three authors are **already recorded**; what is
> new is **how he ranks and characterises them.**

## Summary

★★★★ **No new names — but three new characterisations**, including a **lineage claim** about Jim Rohn
and a **sixth dated Blair Enns attestation.**

## The three, in his order

### ★★★★ 1. Jim Rohn — *Seven Strategies for Wealth and Happiness*

> ★★★★ *"An excellent holistic view from **THE GODFATHER OF COACHES.** **Jim Rohn has been the mentor
> for EVERYBODY THAT YOU LOOK UP TO TODAY** and the authors [whom] you read. **His most notable student
> or mentee is a guy named ANTHONY ROBBINS** — I don't know if you've heard of him."*

✅ **Jim Rohn is recorded as a recurring influence; this is the first time the corpus has his standing
and lineage** — *godfather of coaches*, mentor to the field, with **Tony Robbins named as his student.**
⚠️ **A claim about other people's lineage**, not about himself; recorded as his characterisation.

### ★★★★★ 2. Michael Bungay Stanier — *The Coaching Habit*

> ★★★★★ *"A **surprising** book… I think it's meant to help people become better coaches and leaders,
> but I think it's **an excellent framework for understanding THE VALUE OF ASKING GREAT QUESTIONS,
> BEING SLOWER TO ANSWER, AND STAYING CURIOUS A LITTLE BIT LONGER.** And it so happens to **OVERLAP
> WITH HOW YOU DO SALES.**"*

✅ **This is a stated source for the ask-don't-tell discipline** — and note the three components, which
the corpus has separately but never as a set: **ask well · answer slower · stay curious longer.** He is
already credited for *"so, what's on your mind"*; **this makes the debt structural rather than a single
opener.**

### ★★★★ 3. Blair Enns — *The Win Without Pitching Manifesto*

★★★★ *"**No surprise to anybody who's been following me**… it's **a great POSITIONING book for people
who SELL EXPERT SERVICES.**"*

✅ **A sixth dated attestation** (2017 ×3, 2018, 2023, now 2025) — and the first that classifies the
book by **category**: not a sales book, **a positioning book.** *"No surprise to anybody who's been
following me"* is itself evidence of how consistently he has credited it.
