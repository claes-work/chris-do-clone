# What If GREATNESS Isn't Enough For Success? (Daily Dose: Vlog 004) — 2025-06-03

## Summary

Short vlog, unusually high density. Three things in it matter: a **dated business update
in which Chris says he spun a portion of The Futur off to his CEO**, a fully-argued case
that **social metrics are now used as a proxy for business competence** ("social media is
the new resume"), and a closing indictment of creatives who refuse to publish. Plus live
coaching notes on pitching that extend the presentation material from Vlog 003.

## Key claims (2025-06-03)

### ★★★ Business update — The Futur restructured, and Chris says he is lighter for it

Told plainly, in one pass, unprompted:

- *"**This year has been a really good year of rebuilding**, but there's been a lot of
  momentum. **It's just financially it's not reflective of momentum.**"*
- *"I'm also on a **TV show**, and they made me an **executive producer** of the TV show.
  Things are just lining up."*
- The spin-off, in his own account: *"I came back from **Taki's mastermind** and I called
  up my CEO and I said, **'I got a crazy idea. Are you open-minded to spinning off this
  other group that you can just run — and just taking a portion of the company with you?'**
  He goes, 'Um, yeah.' So **he has his own company now. He took the coaching portion of the
  coaching community with him.**"*
- The verdict he gives it: *"So **we're down the revenue** from that. **But we're lighter
  because of the overhead.** And I'm just lighter because **we're such a small team now.
  It's mostly on the content side.** So I just feel much lighter, much more aligned,
  happier, more energetic. **I'm loving everything.**"*

### ★★★ Social media is the new résumé

- The premise, stated about himself first: *"I always tell people **I have outsized
  opportunities relative to myself** — because of one key metric that people keep looking
  for."*
- Where he got it: at a large conference, in a room of people successful *"in influence or
  management or business related to influencers"* — *"they said **social media is the new
  resume, because resumes, people lie. If you have trash followers, they could just tell.**
  But you post trash content, like — you're not it."*
- **The worked example**, which is what gives it teeth: he helped a friend run an event and
  recommended speakers. *"He goes, 'I can't put her up.' I'm like, 'How come? They're
  amazing.' — **'Their social following is trash.'** So when I announce them, the people who
  are going to buy a ticket are like, why would we? **They're not going to go chase a rabbit
  hole. They're not going to go 'oh, there's a hidden TED talk over here.' They just see:
  oh my god, this is a big person — or this is a nothing.**"*
- His judgement on it is ambivalent, not celebratory: *"it's a **weird world** in which
  we're living in… we live in a very different world now."*
- **The prescription:** *"As much as you put into whatever it is you're doing, **I would
  dedicate at least 10% of your time, your energy, your life force into figuring out how to
  do this social thing.** It's **not about selling or building funnels.** It's just about
  how to create content that connects with people to grow your community. **And if you can
  do that, it doesn't even matter if your products are superior or inferior. You will crush
  everyone.**"*
- The wider frame: *"We're living in an increasingly **media-aware** world. Some people are
  saying **don't even say social media any more — just say media**, where anybody can reach
  anyone in the world. And the competitions that I'm a part of, **they use the social metric
  and engagement as a barometer for business acumen, for marketing skill.** So **we can no
  longer be safe and feel comfortable just running our business well.**"*

### ★★★ The closing indictment — refusal as active participation

> *"I can say this so I'm blue in the face. But **so many of you are sitting on your hands,
> artificially crippling your own business and your own growth** — not only professionally,
> but personally — **because you're not willing to put content out there.** I know what
> you're thinking: I don't know what to say. I'm going to alienate people. I hate the sound
> of my voice or the way I look on camera. **That amount of resistance or reluctance that
> you're feeling IS the thing that's holding you back** from staying at this level and
> going on to many levels above that. I just want you to sit with that and know that this
> is what you're actively participating in… because **when you're not activating your
> brand's potential, you're participating in its own demise.**"*

### Pitch judging — the three-way balance

Observing the finalists in the green room:

- *"Everybody was in different states of preparedness and confidence. **Sometimes the
  person that you see in the green room, you would pick to win — and then they go on stage
  and they totally crumble.** And sometimes they're exactly who they are, but that's not
  what the judges are looking for. **So it's a mixed bag.**"*
- The framework: *"you're trying to strike that balance of **relatability and ambition and
  professionalism.**"* With both failure modes named:
  - too professional → *"they think **maybe you don't need help**, and you're not very
    relatable"*;
  - too relatable, no substance → *"if you're **a hot mess** and you don't know anything but
    you're highly relatable, if they give you the money they might think: **what's this
    person going to do with it?**"*
- What the judges are actually doing: *"**we're voting with our money** to see who we think
  should win and **who we believe in the most to go to the next level.**"*

### Coaching his mentee (live notes)

- *"When you're first starting, it's kind of like **you're being too cute.** Once you got
  into the rhythm of your story, I think **you settled in.**"*
- *"Just make sure you **slow down**, announce it, and you're good."*
- *"**Don't worry about finishing early. It's no problem… They'll remember more.**"*
  ✅ Consistent with the *"say less… instead of remembering you, they can't remember
  anything"* rule from [[2025-05-25-yt-V5c1YGTzAYc|Vlog 003]] eight days earlier.
- *"**If you just be you, you're super relatable.** You got a good story. You're going to do
  just fine."*
