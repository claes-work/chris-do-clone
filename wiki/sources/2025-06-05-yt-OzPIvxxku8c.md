# How NOT to Build a Brand (STOP Being Fake) w/ Jodie Cook (2025-06-05)

> **Attribution:** ✅ **Chris answers the substantive questions.** The guest opens by asking him to
> critique *her* brand, and he redirects — *"that was a long way of saying we're gonna talk about you
> today"* — but the definitional material below is his. Her questions and her own reflections are
> `[HOST]`.

## Summary

★★★★ **A two-part TEST for whether a personal brand is real** — the corpus has definitions of personal
brand but not a test — plus the clearest statement that **followers are not the metric**, and a cold
open on identity worth carrying to `voice`.

## Key claims (2025-06-05)

### ★★★★★ The cold open

> ★★★★★ *"**The most natural thing to do — and the MOST DIFFICULT thing to do in this world — is TO
> KNOW WHO YOU ARE AND TO BE YOURSELF.** This is kind of **a GRIEVING**, because **it's almost like
> THE OLD YOU HAS TO DIE so the new you can come out.** … Somewhere along the way you're like: **but
> that ain't me. I don't fit in that neat little box — AND I DON'T WANT TO FIT IN A BOX. I WANT TO
> EXPLODE THE BOX.** I want to do my thing. I want to break all the rules."* → and the line that
> follows: *"**I totally know who put me in my box.**"*

✅ Reads directly onto the **thank-and-retire-the-old-self** material — and adds **grief** as the
emotional cost.

### ★★★★ The definition, extended past the definition

★★★★ *"I really do like **Marty Neumeier's** definition of brand — **a person's gut feeling about a
product, service or organisation.** So **it's not something that we can WHOLLY CONTROL. We can
INFLUENCE, we can NUDGE a little bit.**"* → **the extension:** *"we all have [one] by that definition,
**because your parents think something of you. Your siblings think something of you. Your children
think something of you. AND WHEN ENOUGH PEOPLE COME TO A SIMILAR GUT FEELING, THEN YOU CAN SAY THAT
YOU HAVE A BRAND.**"*

★★★ **The modern qualifier:** *"in the 21st century, with the rise of the social platforms and
everybody making media to a degree, **we are able to INCREASE THE REACH AND ENGAGEMENT of that
potential gut feeling.**"*

### ★★★★★ Followers are not the metric

> ★★★★★ *"**You can have a lot of followers, in my opinion, and NOT have a very good personal brand.
> And FOLLOWERS IS NOT A GREAT METRIC for whether you have any real influence.**"*

★★★★ **Two worked counter-examples:**
- **Faceless content** — writing plus voiceover, or fully AI-generated: *"**you may have THREE MILLION
  FOLLOWERS AND NO ONE KNOWS WHO YOU ARE.**"*
- **Curation accounts** — a lot of followers from reposting good viral content: *"**I still don't know
  who you are, and you're not able to actually MOVE THE NEEDLE.**"*

### ★★★★★ The two-part test — is the brand real?

> ★★★★★ **1. Can you make kings or queens?** *"**by virtue of you being around other people, THEY
> become a little bit more influential BECAUSE OF THEIR PROXIMITY TO YOU. That's super powerful.**"*
>
> ★★★★★ **2. Can you print money?** *"a person who has a really strong personal brand… **they have the
> ability to PRINT MONEY. Not literally — but figuratively.**"*

✅ **This is a TEST, not a definition** — and the corpus currently carries several definitions of
personal brand and no test. It also reframes the goal: **not being known, but being able to confer.**

### ★★★★ The posture he takes as the interviewee

> ★★★★ *"You are going to tell me **everything I'm doing wrong** — slash how I should think about doing
> it right — **and I'm going to be A WILLING STUDENT. I'M GOING TO HIDE NOTHING. I'm going to be
> completely open with everything.** And sometimes you're going to ask me really big questions and
> I'm going to ask the smaller questions, and **SOMETIMES I'M NOT GOING TO HAVE A CLUE, and sometimes
> I'm going to know what I'm talking about.**"*

✅ Consistent with *"I'm a really good student"* ([[2024-04-30-yt-3AS0gfiG63Y|2024-04-30]]) and
*"I know when I'm out of my league"* ([[2021-03-23-yt-QoMPxUWMo8g|2021-03-23]]).
