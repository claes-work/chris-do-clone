# "You're too EXPENSIVE" (short, 2025-06-24)

> **Attribution:** ✅ Chris, giving a verbatim script.

## Summary

★★★★★ **The corpus has a great deal on pricing and objections, and no script for the single most
common one.** This gives it — and the move is **not a defence of the price.**

## The script

> ★★★★★ *"If your prospect says '**that's too expensive**', here's what I would say: **'I'M SORRY. I
> THINK I MISJUDGED THE PROBLEM WE'RE TRYING TO SOLVE. I thought we were trying to solve a really big
> problem that had an impact on your business…'**"*

✅ **Note what the move actually does — three things at once:**
1. **It concedes nothing about the price.** The number is not defended, discounted or justified.
2. **It relocates the disagreement to the PROBLEM.** *"Too expensive"* is treated as evidence that
   **the problem was mis-sized**, not that the fee was.
3. **It takes the error onto himself** — *"I misjudged"* — which leaves the client free to correct him
   upward rather than defend a low number.

⚠️ **The transcript is cut before the completion**, so the ending is **not reproduced**. What is
recorded is the opening move, which is the part that is unusual.

✅ **Consistent with the diagnosis at [[2025-11-24-yt-UhklGQya1r4|2025-11-24]]** — *"anytime you can't
sell something and it's not worth a lot, it's because they don't feel the pain"* — **this is that
diagnosis converted into a sentence you can say out loud.**
