# The Secret Storytelling Hack Every Business Needs! — w/ Greg Logan (2025-08-07)

> **⚠️ ATTRIBUTION — GUEST-DOMINATED.** The guest is **Greg Logan**, founder of **Narrativity**, who
> adapts screenwriting structures into brand storytelling. **The method is entirely his** and is
> summarised below **as context only**. Chris's contributions are marked **[CHRIS]**.
>
> **But this episode has an unusual feature:** Logan runs his **live brand exercise on The Futur
> itself**, which makes Chris articulate his own audience, value and mission on camera. That section
> is subject-attributed and is the reason the page exists.

## Summary

A storytelling-consultancy episode that turns, halfway through, into **a live diagnostic performed on
Chris**. The harvest is small but genuinely valuable: **his own statement of who he serves and what he
does for them**, and — when pushed one level deeper — **a philosophical claim about creativity and
childhood** that the corpus has only in fragments elsewhere.

## ★★★★ Chris on his own audience and value (the live exercise)

**Who:** *"I would say **creative entrepreneurs** — **not just creatives, because they need different
kinds of help.** And **the market of entrepreneurs is too big.** So: creative entrepreneurs."*

**What he does for them**, given unrehearsed and in order:

> **[CHRIS]** *"**I teach them to speak the language of business.** … **I help them to have a
> healthier mindset around money.** … **I help them to find their worth — not just in financial means,
> but in terms of the goodness that they put into the world.**"*

**And the benefit under the benefit, when pushed:**

> **[CHRIS]** *"They create **a sustainable business — the one that they love, the one that they show
> up for** — the reason why they **avoided the traditional path and decided to pursue a creative
> life.** And the true benefit is **they're able to create a world for themselves in which they don't
> feel the financial pressure of having to be at the beck and call of clients** — that they can **help
> their family and loved ones and live a life of creativity. Because it's hard to do that these
> days.**"*

### ★★★★ The philosophy he sidesteps into

Asked for a sharper word than *creativity*, he declines the wordsmithing and goes underneath it
instead — *"now I'm going to get philosophical on you. I'm going to sidestep the question a little
bit"*:

> *"I have this **fundamental belief that all of us are creative — non-creatives as well.** And
> **somewhere along the way, the creativity was taught out of us.** And **the artists in the world are
> the ones who survived childhood and didn't go along with the program. They're the rebels. They're
> the non-conformists.**"*

✅ This is the belief underneath *"the things that have been taught out of you"* in the
[[2025-07-29-yt-hDlWbmH96hw|VidCon keynote]] — stated here as a claim about **who artists are**, not
just about what schooling removes.

## Greg Logan's method (guest — context only, NOT persona data)

- Adapted from film: **27 movie genres**, each mapped to a brand meaning; plus an *enemy* and a
  *superpower*.
- **The quest** — a single sentence stating *"the very biggest end benefit to the customer, so they
  go: **I want to see that achieved**"* — which is also *"a really great thing to make decisions
  against in your business."* His favourite example, from a hospice client: *"**to give people their
  best death possible.**"*
- **Co-creation, not delivery**: *"I don't take that brief and come back weeks or months later and
  tell them what I think they should say. **We create it together.** … **They know their business
  better than me. Far better than me.**"*
- **The banned-words list**, given to clients before work starts — *"they freak out, because they go:
  those are all the words we say. And I go: yeah, **but you don't realise that everyone else is saying
  the same thing.**"* On it: **trust** (*"if anyone says trust me, you don't trust them. **What is it
  you do that makes people trust you?**"*), **journey** (*"put any category in front of journey…
  wellness journey, financial journey, podcast journey"*), **innovation**, **passion**,
  **customer-focused** — and he adds **authentic** during the conversation.
- **Against the shopping list**: *"stop talking about yourself. **Stop putting in a shopping list,
  because no one can remember it.** … The more stuff you put in, **it emotionally distances people
  from you, because you're just talking longer and longer about yourself.**"*
