# Every Dollar Is a Vote (short, 2025-08-19)

> **Attribution:** ✅ Chris. ⚠️ **Contains a dated commercial opinion about a named public figure and
> his company, plus a personal disclosure. Both are recorded as stated — see the guard below.**

## Summary

★★★★ **Buying as identity, with a credited quote** — and an unusually direct application: **when you
ask for a subscribe, a comment or a sale, you must be able to say what else the person is buying.**
