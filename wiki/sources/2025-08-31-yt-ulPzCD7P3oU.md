# 2X Your Income As a Solopreneur (short, 2025-08-31)

> **Attribution:** ✅ Chris, teaching a method with arithmetic on screen.

## Summary

★★★★★ **A worked costing method the corpus does not have.** It argues for higher rates constantly and
**never says how to find the market rate or how to build a number up.** This does both, with numbers.

## The method, in order

### ★★★★★ 1. Find the market rate — a stated procedure

> ★★★★★ *"**First, find out what the market is paying for people like you.** What you want to do is
> **find THREE PEOPLE who are AT OR ABOVE YOUR LEVEL** and find out **what their rates are and what
> they're charging.** Then **just average the three together** — add all three up and divide by three,
> **and that's going to be the rate.**"*

⚠️⚠️ **CORRECTED 2026-07-28.** This originally claimed the corpus had no method for discovering a market
rate. **That was wrong** — a broken phrase-check returned a false zero. *At or above your level* is
**already recorded** in `topics/pricing`, `topics/business`, `persona/beliefs` and the compiled prompt.
✅ **What this source adds is the ARITHMETIC** — *find three, average them* — and the constraint stated
explicitly: **at or above, not peers, not below.**

### ★★★★★ 2. Price as if you were not doing the work

> ★★★★★ *"Now I want you to **ACT AS IF YOU COULD NOT ACTUALLY DO ANY OF THE WORK YOURSELF** — and that
> all you're doing is **managing the client relationships and the project itself.**"*

✅ **This is the load-bearing move.** It converts a rate for *your time* into the cost of *delivering
the outcome*, which is what makes the number defensible — and it is the same logic as the
delegation/leverage material, applied to pricing.

### ★★★★ 3. Build it up — with his own worked example

*"Take **the rate you'd need to pay someone to do the work × the time it takes them**, and **add a
project-management fee.**"*

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Designer, 8 days @ \$1,000/day | **\$8,000** |
| Project management | **\$3,500** |
| **Subtotal — break-even** | **\$11,500** |
| Profit @ 30% | **\$3,450** |
| **Total** | **\$14,950** |

★★★★★ **And the point he stresses:** *"**this is NOT where we stop, because this is just to BREAK
EVEN.** … **You need to add PROFIT.** … **This is the real profit of a company when you account for the
expense of how it's going to be produced.**"*

★★★ **The claim:** *"do this and you'll **double your income**."* ⚠️ Recorded as stated; **no evidence
is offered in the short.**
