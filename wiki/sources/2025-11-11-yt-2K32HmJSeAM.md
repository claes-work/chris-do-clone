# Embrace Your Imperfect Self! (short, 2025-11-11)

> **Attribution:** ✅ Chris, closing a live event from stage.

## Summary

★★★★ **A different AI-moat mechanism from the one the corpus records.** Elsewhere the moat is **taste**
and **judgement**; here it is **live interpersonal friction** — and he attaches an instruction that
undercuts the event he is standing at.

## Key claims (2025-11-11)

> ★★★★ *"The thing I love the most about each and every single one of you is **YOUR DIRTY IMPERFECT
> SELF.** And the thing that **AI WILL NEVER REPLACE is THE WEIRD STICKY TENSION that happens in a
> space like this** — that for now, unless you're a cyborg, **THE REAL IN-PERSON EXPERIENCE is what the
> machine cannot replace.**"*

✅ **The corpus records taste-as-moat repeatedly** (and, at
[[2026-05-09-yt-awg3uUYedOg|2026-05-09]], how he built his). **This names a different thing entirely**:
not a skill AI lacks, but **a condition — being in a room — that AI cannot instantiate.**

★★★★ **The instruction, and it is self-undercutting in a way worth preserving:** *"I know a lot of you
are **socially awkward, weird, introverts JUST LIKE MYSELF** — **go talk to somebody today.** Just go
talk to them, because **that's the relationship that you're going to build from this point forward that
might change your life.** **NOT THE PANEL. NOT WHAT YOU HEAR — because you're not going to do anything
anyways. GO TALK TO THREE PEOPLE.**"*

> ✅ **He tells an audience that the content they came for is not the valuable part** — including his
> own panel — and gives a countable action instead. ⚠️ *"You're not going to do anything anyways"* is
> **not cynicism about them**; it is the same realism as *"if you can do one tenth, one fiftieth of
> what she's done"* ([[2020-11-17-yt-GV9fj-Cud5U|2020-11-17]]).

★★★ **And the self-inclusion** — *"introverts just like myself"* — consistent with the introversion
account in [[../persona/biography|biography]].
