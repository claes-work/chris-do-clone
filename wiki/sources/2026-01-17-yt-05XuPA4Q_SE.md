# How to Build Content People Actually React To (2026-01-17)

> **Attribution:** a live content-review session. Chris runs the diagnostic; the people whose content
> is reviewed are **members of the public who submitted it** and are `[SUBMITTER]` — **not named
> here.** A co-host assists.

## Summary

★★★★★ **A live demonstration of the diagnostic itself, narrated as it happens.** He stops mid-session
to tell the audience what he is doing and why — *"notice we've not talked about any tactics"* — which
makes this the clearest worked example in the corpus of **outcome → metric → baseline → benchmark**,
before any advice is given.

## Key claims (2026-01-17)

### ★★★★★ He names the method mid-session — and what he is deliberately not doing

> ★★★★★ *"**I'm going to take a little bit longer to talk to you versus just answering your question
> about content — because I'M GOING TO HELP A LOT OF CREATIVE PEOPLE UNDERSTAND HOW TO TALK TO
> POTENTIAL CLIENTS, or have A STRATEGIC BUSINESS CONVERSATION. NOTICE WE'VE NOT TALKED ABOUT ANY
> TACTICS. I just want to know WHAT'S IMPORTANT TO YOU.**"*

✅ **The fourth-wall break as a teaching device again** — and here it converts a content critique into
a **client-conversation demonstration**, which he says is the actual point.

### ★★★★★ The sequence, visible step by step

1. ★★★★★ **Outcome first, not content:** *"**what is THE OUTCOME that you want to get from the creation
   of content? What is it you're trying to do?**"*
2. ★★★★ **Write it down as an equation** — on his notebook, held up to camera: *"custom trophy shoes
   **=** fans love brands."* ✅ Reduces a business to one line before anything is measured.
3. ★★★★★ **Then ask how the outcome is measured:** *"**how do we MEASURE the amount of love that fans
   show to brands?**"* → views, comments, shares.
4. ★★★★★ **BASELINE AND BENCHMARK**, defined plainly: *"**what is the BASELINE and what's the
   BENCHMARK? Baseline is WHAT'S THE AVERAGE — and then WHAT WOULD YOU LIKE TO GET IT TO.**"* → in this
   case, baseline **5,000 views**, success **10,000+**.
5. ★★★★★ **He refuses to pick the metric for them:** *"**you tell me what matters to you and I'll write
   it down.**"* → and when pressed: *"**I don't really care which one you use. For the sake of our
   conversation, PICK ONE AND LET'S STICK WITH IT.**"*

★★★★ **And he affirms a modest number rather than pushing for ambition:** *"**that's very realistic,
actually. So I like that you said five.**"* ✅ Notable — the diagnostic is calibrated to their reality,
not to his.

★★★★ **Then he looks for the outliers, not the average:** noting that the pinned posts have roughly a
million views apiece, he asks **why those are abnormally high** — because the gap between baseline and
outlier is where the explanation lives.

### ★★★★★ He catches himself leading the witness — in the moment

> ★★★★★ *"Now, are those that high? Because — well, **SORRY, LET ME NOT LEAD THE WITNESS. Sorry, your
> honor — objection, leading the witness.** Um — **why are those abnormally high?**"*

✅ **A documented self-correction on question technique**, made aloud and mid-sentence. It is the
ask-don't-tell discipline being **enforced by him on himself**, and it is funnier and more convincing
than the rule stated abstractly. ✅ Pairs with *"I'm stopping myself from talking right there"*
([[2016-02-13-yt-pKMfPGAZHYI|2016-02-13]]) — **the same habit, a decade apart.**
