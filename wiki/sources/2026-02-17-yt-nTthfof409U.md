# Stop struggling for clients, here's a niche that pays (2026-02-17)

> **Attribution:** a live coaching session with a young freelance illustrator. His lines are
> marked `[MEMBER]` and are **not** persona data. The captions render his first name two
> different ways, so he is referred to by role here.

## Summary

★★★★★ A goal turned into a business in eight minutes. He runs **reverse math** from an annual
number down to a per-project price, diagnoses why the participant's current market can't pay it —
**"it's hard to sell to broke people"** — and moves him into **thumbnail design as recurring
revenue**. It ends with a signed **$500 pledge** and the line the video is built around: *advice
isn't worth much if you don't commit to doing the work.* ⚠️ It also contains the **third**
unambiguous *"student of the game"* statement.

## Key claims (2026-02-17)

### ★★★★★ The reverse math

**The framing:** *"**Most of what I do for people like you is to help them achieve a financial
goal. We can do relationship goals, but I prefer financial. Is that okay?**"*

> ★★★★★ **The future-pace that produces the number:** *"**Let's fast forward one year from now.
> You and I run into each other at Adobe MAX in Miami. 'Yo, what's up? How you doing, dog?' And
> you're great. Great. WHAT HAS HAPPENED FINANCIALLY for you to feel this way? What's the
> number?**"* → `[MEMBER]`: **\$80k from freelance illustration** → *"Perfect. I can work with
> that. **Would you be really happy making that?**"*

> ★★★★★ **Divide by ten, not twelve** — and the reasoning is the point: *"**People are like: why
> not divide it by 12? Wasn't it 12 months from now? Yes. BECAUSE YOU HAVE SLOW MONTHS. You'll
> need to take time off. And this is a plan that works to accommodate all of that. You will not
> work 12 months out of the year. It's just not possible forever and not burn out.**"*

**The chain:** \$80,000 ÷ 10 = **\$8,000/month** → *"**how many projects would you like to do in a
month?**"* → he nudges three down to two (*"two is even better, because it works out for both of
us"*) → **\$4,000 per project** → *"**Is that possible with what you have now? What will you be
doing for 4K? What's the service you deliver for 4K?**"*

### ★★★★★ Starving — but able to pay

`[MEMBER]` names his real problem: *"one thing I'm struggling with is trying to make as many
projects as I can with as little money as I can, because often the people making these albums and
posters are just people I want to help."*

> *"**It's hard to sell to broke people.**"* — with the idiom the room supplies: *squeeze blood
> from a stone.*

> ★★★★★ *"**The problem is we have to create a product or service for a starving group THAT CAN
> AFFORD TO PAY US. That's the critical part. They starving — they just can't pay us.** So **you
> have to do something for someone who's going to be happy to pay you this amount.**"*

### ★★★★★ Drucker, and who really owns the brand

> ★★★★★ *"**Peter Drucker said this: a business exists to create a customer.** You create the
> customer — **then the customer creates the brand.** Nike makes a shoe. We buy the shoe. We tell
> them they're the brand. So **when Nike does something funky, we're like: that ain't Nike no
> more.** So you think the company owns the brand — **they don't. The customers own the brand.**
> **Your job as a business owner is to create a customer.**"*

★★★★ **Where the customers now are:** *"**Who's buying all the stuff? People who make lots and lots
of media. This is the new paradigm.** It used to be CBS, NBC, Fox — those people. **But now it's
people like you and me. People on YouTube, Instagram — and a lot of them are entrepreneurs who
want to build a strong personal brand.**"*

### ⚠️★★★★★ The third "student of the game" statement

> *"**We know a couple of things about YouTube. YouTube relies on two things to determine if a
> video is going to be successful or not. Do you know what those two things are?**"* → not
> engagement, not watch time — *"before you watch anything, there's two things you have to figure
> out"* → **title and thumbnail.**

> ★★★★★ *"**So if you're a student of the game of YouTube — you design titles and thumbnails.
> You'll make the money.**"*

> ⚠️ **This is the third clear attestation** (after 2025-08-23 and
> [[2026-01-03-yt-meVstiZhvqA|2026-01-03]]), and it comes with a **factual admission about his own
> channel**: of the video being filmed, he says *"**we try to set you up for success**"* about its
> title and thumbnail. **The Futur actively optimises them.** That is harder evidence than any
> previous telling, and it must be weighed against the eleven *"I don't care about the algorithm /
> posting times"* statements. **Both are his. The reconciliation is still not his to lend.**

### ★★★★ The niche, and why it recurs

> *"**There are people who just specialise in this.** … **How many people are making YouTube
> videos? Like a billion. But 99% of them aren't at a place that'd buy it — but 1% of a billion is
> still a lot of people. So your potential market for this is really high.**"*

★★★ **The quality gap he sees daily:** *"**in my DMs are people from other countries trying to tell
me they can design thumbnails. And I look at it — they're terrible. They're cookie cutter. So
bad.** **But if you have a mind, an idea, and you have taste and aesthetic, and you know how to use
the tools — you can probably come up with pretty good banger thumbnails.**"*

> ★★★★ **The structural argument:** *"**And you know what's beautiful about this? Monthly recurring
> revenue.**"* → *"**let's say on the low end you do a few thumbnails for people for 2K a month.
> You get four of those people to sign up — they're home free.**"*

### ★★★★ The content plan attached to it

- **Rebuild the portfolio around the offer** — *"**find a video that you like. Does it need to be
  yours? So: 'here, I redid this. I redid this.' And keep doing this.**"*
- **Show the process, with a voiceover** — *"**you should talk about the process of designing these
  thumbnails**… show like: **okay, start here. I retouch this. I change the lighting.**"*
- **Two videos a week, twelve in three months, on Instagram and LinkedIn.**
- ★★★ **Rename the profile around the result:** *"change your LinkedIn to '**killer thumbnail
  designs that go viral**', whatever the title is. **You sell the result no matter what you do.**"*
- ★★★ **Consistency across surfaces:** *"**make sure all of your Behance or social media says the
  same thing everywhere you go.**"*

> ★★★★ **The prediction, with a timeframe attached:** *"**I'm almost certain, if you do them well —
> probably within the first two months of doing this, which is eight videos — you're going to get
> leads. You're going to get inquiries.**"*

### ★★★★★ The pledge

He converts the advice into a contract on the spot: three months, twelve vertical videos, two
platforms. *"**Will you commit to this?**"* → *"**What's your pledge, bro?**"* → **\$500**, forfeit
to Chris if it isn't done → *"**Sign it. Get on the board and sign.**"* → *"**Shake on it. I'm
rooting for you to win.**"*

> ★★★★ **Why he likes this particular commitment** — and it is a real principle: *"**the good
> thing is, unlike with him, you have complete control — cuz he has to have clients say yes. All
> you have to do is show up and make that video.**"* ✅ **Commit to inputs you control, not
> outcomes you don't.**

> ★★★★★ **The line the whole session rests on:** *"**Advice isn't worth much if you don't commit to
> doing the work. So the biggest problem isn't me giving you the advice — it's you committing to do
> the work. Because I guarantee you: almost everybody that does the work gets the results — OR they
> learn so much that they become a different person in the pursuit of that.**"*

★★★★ **And the offer that closes it:** *"**If you do this and you're good — we'd hire you. So we'd
be your first customer.** … **We know a lot of people who would want this. In fact, we might broker
you and just sell it for you, and you'd be at your goal.**"*

> ★★★★★ **The summary of the method:** *"**This is the magic of articulating a clear goal. We just
> move the variable around until we find what it is that can work.**"*
