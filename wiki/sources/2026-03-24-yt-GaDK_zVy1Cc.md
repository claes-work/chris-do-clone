# Don't chase virality (do this instead) (2026-03-24)

> **Attribution:** a **keynote at the 1 Billion Followers Summit in Dubai**, delivered as a
> deliberate rebuttal to a preceding talk by **Brendan Kane** — a friend, named with affection
> throughout (*"Brendan, with all apologies"* · *"we're friends, guys… at least up until today"*).
> Audience answers are marked `[ROOM]` and are **not** persona data.

## Summary

★★★★★ **A landmark.** A full-length argument that virality is a **bad goal** — structured as a
decision tree in which *both* outcomes are bad — supported by the **VidCon 2022 empty-rooms
story**, a friend's viral post read comment by comment, and four named mechanisms (**seduction,
addiction, prison, awakening**). It then inverts the tree with a better goal, argues **quantity
over quality** from *Art & Fear*, lays out **the three circles** on a napkin, proposes a
**gift economy**, and ends with The Futur's own numbers. ⚠️ It is also the single most important
source yet for the standing **algorithm contradiction**.

## Key claims (2026-03-24)

### ★★★★★ The correct contrarian

Credited to **Mark Manson**: *"if you want to be a 1%-er — **and I don't think he's talking about
the top 1% of the richest people in the world, but the people who make the greatest impact** —
they all do this one thing. **They are contrarians.**"*

> ★★★★★ *"**But any fool can go a different direction. So you have to be a CORRECT CONTRARIAN.**
> You have to find something that goes against what the mainstream is saying — **whatever it is
> that you strongly believe in your heart. Not to do it because it's a gimmick, but because you
> believe in your heart this isn't correct. Because if you say what everyone else is saying,
> you're going to disappear in all the noise.**"*

> ★★★★ **With a disclaimer about himself:** *"**I take this very personally, because I am NOT
> TRYING to be contrarian — but I have very strong points of view on lots of things.**"*

### ★★★★★ The "fast" chain

Fast fashion → fast food → easy money → crash diets → **fast fans.**

- ⚠️ **Fast fashion:** *"they not only destroy legitimate fashion brands by knocking off their
  designs, but they're producing things at such a rapid pace that **they are the most harmful
  companies to the environment, even more than big oil**"* — *a strong claim, recorded as his* —
  and the detail that lands it: *"**it's become cheaper to replace the clothes than it is to wash
  the clothes. That's scary to me.**"*
- **Fast food:** *"good for shelf life, not good for your body."*
- ★★★★★ **Easy money:** *"**get rich quick, you also lose it really quick.** A very high percentage
  of people who win the lottery wind up right back where they started. I wonder why. **Because
  maybe, just maybe, THERE IS SOMETHING TO THE PURSUIT — of learning something, of working hard —
  THAT IS MORE DESIRABLE THAN THE RESULT ITSELF.**"*
- **Crash diets:** *"**the hint is in the title itself: CRASH. Maybe the word DIE.**"* — and the
  mechanism: *"**because you see it as a very short-term goal to hit, once you hit the goal you
  regress — because those habits haven't been formed inside of you.**"*
- ★★★★★ **Fast fans:** engagement farms — *"**you pay, you get the views. As soon as you stop
  paying, you don't get the views.**"* → *"**I would rather have a hundred people who are dedicated,
  who truly want to consume my content, than a million people who are there because somebody's
  clicking it in some country.**"*

> ★★★★ **The title:** *"**we came out of a pandemic. I'm not so keen on viruses myself. So the
> antidote to this idea of going viral is to be ANTIVIRAL.**"* → the talk is *"**The Dark Side of
> Virality: why easy success might become a prison sentence.**"*

### ★★★★★ Three words, three meanings

He makes the room supply them:

| Goal | What it's actually called |
|---|---|
| **Awareness** | *"It's not just called content creation. **It's called MARKETING.**"* |
| **Conversion** | *"**It's called SALES.**"* |
| **Trust** | *"**BRANDING.**"* |

> ★★★★★ *"**What I don't like is when we use one term to describe too many things — then the words
> lose meaning. And we have to be specific about the words that we use, BECAUSE THE WORDS SHAPE OUR
> WORLDS.**"* ✅ The same phrase as [[2025-12-31-yt-POnPkjjT6Pw|2025-12-31]].

### ★★★★★ The decision tree where both branches are bad

**If you DO go viral:** reach, engagement, follows, monetisation — *"we're really excited about
making more content cuz we're rewarded for it. **That's how the algorithms sink their hooks into
you.**"* → *"we went viral. **We need to go viral again.**"* →

> ★★★★★ *"Then we enter into this trap. **It's called the VALIDATION LOOP — where we're looking
> outside to be validated versus looking inwards. Extrinsic versus intrinsic.** So even at your
> best possible outcome, **you get what you want — but now you're stuck.**"*

**If you DON'T:** *"**now I'm not really motivated to make content anymore. And my ego, my
self-esteem, my self-worth, my self-value takes a hit. And sometimes it leads to depression. And if
you do this for long enough, it leads to BURNOUT.** … **eventually burnout leads to you
quitting.**"*

> ★★★★★ *"**So I presented to you a very structured argument why NEITHER OPTION IS GOOD.** … **The
> problem with virality is IT'S BASED ON AN OUTCOME.**"*

★★★★ **His working definition:** *"**it means you have an OUTLIER. Something that is 10x what you
normally produce.**"*

★★★★★ **And the live poll that settles it.** Hands up if you've gone viral → *"**how many were able
to REPEAT it?**"* (hands drop) → *"**how many have been able to SUSTAIN it, over and over
again?**"* → *"**Only a handful of people.**"*

### ★★★★★ Problem 1 — The seduction

> ★★★★★ *"**Virality feels very OPAQUE. None of the platforms tell you how to go viral. So there's
> all these professional gurus, coaches and programs telling you what they GUESS it is — even
> though the platforms do not tell you.** … **And I think what they give, they can take away.**"*

> ★★★★★ **The principled objection, and the best statement of it in the corpus:** *"**I personally
> do not like to EXPORT MY EMOTIONAL WELL-BEING to a machine — to the corporate overlords.** …
> **What we used to look inside for, to know whether we're doing the right thing — now we're
> trusting a machine that we do not understand, from our corporate overlords who never talk to us,
> to regulate our emotional well-being. I don't like this at all.**"*

**The Jodie Cook case.** A friend, a Forbes senior contributor: ~155 posts, ~10.7k followers,
typical posts around 2,000 views — then one at **2.9 million**, then straight back to ~4,200.

> ★★★★ **Her question, and his answer:** *"'**Chris, I've never gone viral before. Is this what it
> feels like for you and your life?**' I'm like: '**No. I DON'T GO VIRAL EITHER. I don't know what's
> going on.**'"*

She wrote it up — notifications she can't ignore, an inability to focus, compulsive checking, a
flooded inbox — *"and then there's this other part: **threats, harassment, name-calling and
bullying.**"* He reads the sanitised comments aloud (*"when all you are is money, how poor your life
must be"* · *"these guru prices are out of control"* · one word: *"escort"*).

> ★★★★★ **Why strangers do it:** *"**there's a reason why they say this when you go viral: BECAUSE
> THEY DON'T KNOW YOU. They just see this thing, and they judge you for everything based on one
> thing you said, or the way that you look, or how long your skirt is, or if you wear a baseball
> cap when you talk or not.**"*

> ★★★★★ **His own posture — and it is not stoicism:** *"**I love my bullies. I love the trolls.
> Love the enemy. It's the best approach. I MAKE MORE CONTENT FROM THE BULLIES THAN I DO OF THE
> LOVERS AND THE FANS.** I'll tell you why: **because there's a FRICTION POINT. The internet loves
> friction.** So I have an opportunity to clarify what I meant to say, or how you might have
> misinterpreted this. **And usually it OUTPERFORMS the original post.**"* — plus: *"**you know what
> bullies don't like? Being bullied themselves.**"*

### ★★★★★ Problem 2 — The addiction

> ★★★★★ *"**This whole thing is very close to a perilous DEALER–ADDICT relationship. You're the
> addict, you're not the dealer. I promise you.** … **What do dealers do? They hook you with a
> sample — a viral moment. You feel the high. And when you don't have any more, you experience
> WITHDRAWALS.** You're constantly checking the stats. **It's like scratching: oh, I need another
> fix.**"*

> ★★★★★ **The cruellest part:** *"**The weird thing is — the day before your post went viral, you
> were really happy with yourself, weren't you?** You're making progress. I'm becoming a better
> communicator. I feel better about myself. **SO WHAT USED TO MAKE YOU HAPPY DOESN'T MAKE YOU HAPPY
> ANYMORE — because once you experience this feeling, everything else sucks in comparison.**"*

### ★★★★★ Problem 3 — The prison

> ★★★★★ *"You try to repeat it. **You might have done something really goofy — you might have put
> ice on your face. Now EVERY video you've got to throw ice on your face. So you become a
> CARICATURE OF YOURSELF**, and you no longer feel like it's you who's showing up. **You become a
> slave to the algorithm. This is why it's a prison.**"*

**The authenticity gap, restated with a new self-example:** *"it's the space between the real you —
who you are in private to your very best, most trusted friends — and how you show up in the world.
**I don't sit in the hotel room with my skirt on. It's too uncomfortable. This is how I show up
here. So I AM IN SOME WAY OPERATING AS A PERSONA. I'm wearing a hat. I have a mic. These aren't
normal things I would do if I was sitting in the living room watching TV.**"*

> ★★★★★ *"**What's happening when you go viral? YOU SHOW UP EVEN LESS OF YOURSELF. You become a
> prisoner of your own stupid success, because this is all that people want from you now.** …
> sooner than later **you start wearing this mask and you can't even see yourself anymore.**"*

> ★★★★★ *"**Your interests, your values and your personal growth take a backseat to keeping the
> relationship between you and the dealer happy and stable.**"*

★★★★ **And the punishment for changing:** *"**every time you try something different — you want to
return back to the real you — YOU GET PUNISHED FOR IT.** … They'll leave comments like: **'this
isn't what we followed you for.'** … **This avatar, this thing that you created to go viral,
becomes your prison.**"*

### ★★★★★ Problem 4 — The awakening

> *"**Being viral doesn't mean you've done anything valuable.** … **I think they call it BRAIN ROT
> for a reason. It's not necessarily valuable. It's just what the algorithm wants.**"*

> ★★★★★ **The love bomb:** *"**the algorithm, depending on the platform, rewards you every 10th
> post to give you an artificial boost. They give you a little LOVE BOMB** — 'we love that you're
> on your 10th post, so we're going to juice the numbers for you' — **because they are like drug
> dealers. They want to keep encouraging you enough so you don't quit. BECAUSE THE PLATFORMS WIN
> WHEN MORE PEOPLE CREATE.**"*

→ *"**Bigger reach doesn't mean your message resonates. And followers doesn't equal community.**"*

### ★★★★★ Branding is stickiness — and the loyalty test

> *"**What branding is: it's the STICKINESS of the relationship. It's how loyal people are to
> you.**"*

> ★★★★★ **The test:** *"**all you have to do is ask them to do something simple. Comment this. Go
> vote for me. Nominate me as a speaker for this event. Who knows an organiser who can put me in
> touch with somebody? So you can TEST THE STRENGTH of those relationships — just to see how sticky
> it is.**"*

### ★★★★★ VidCon 2022 — the empty rooms

> ★★★★★ *"Post-pandemic, they open it back up in 2022. TikTok is blowing up like crazy. … **They
> invited some of the biggest TikTokers with millions of followers to come and speak. I WAS AT THAT
> CONFERENCE.** … **Massive rooms for 500 to 800 people. Even for some of the biggest creators that
> you know and love — about 20 TO 30 SEATS. Not percentage. 20 to 30 seats.**"*

He cites a July 2022 **Business Insider** piece on TikTokers struggling to form deep fan
connections, quoting a creator with 1.3 million followers:

> ★★★★★ *"'**When you set up a meet and greet and no one comes — how heartbreaking is that? This
> relationship that you thought you were building wasn't a relationship at all, because you're
> chasing a number.**'"*

> ★★★★★ **His conclusion:** *"**Short-term fame doesn't build lasting bonds. I'd rather have an army
> of 100 like Leonidas have my back than 10,000 people who never show up to help me fight.**"*

### ★★★★★ The inverted tree — a better goal

He rejects two audience answers first: *"**creating raving fans is not the goal. It's a BYPRODUCT of
a goal. Having fun may or may not be the goal — but it could also be a byproduct.**"*

Then the study he heard from **Blair Enns**: a hundred very successful entrepreneurs, each writing
on a post-it what matters to them → *"**they almost all wrote the same answer: THEY WANT TO GROW
PERSONALLY WHILE HELPING OTHER PEOPLE.**"*

> ★★★★★ *"**So maybe the goal in creating content should be PERSONAL DEVELOPMENT — to better
> ourselves while helping other people. That to me is worth more than going viral.**"*

**And the tree run again:**

- **If it works:** *"you feel like you grew. You develop new skills. You become a communicator. You
  build **rhetoric — the ability to persuade people with the spoken word.** **Your identity gets
  more defined. I found my voice in this world. And because I know who I am, I'm more confident and
  I care less about what people say about me.**"*
- ★★★★★ **If it doesn't:** *"**You learn. Because in life, YOU EITHER WIN OR YOU LEARN.** So it
  didn't work. Cool. It's an opportunity to learn. **And if you keep paying attention to the
  lessons, you will eventually get there.**"*

✅ **The structural point is the whole talk in miniature:** with virality, both branches are bad;
with personal development, both branches are good.

### ★★★★★ Quantity — the pottery experiment

From ***Art & Fear*** by **David Bayles and Ted Orland**: one group graded purely on **how much**
pottery they made, the other on **a single masterpiece.**

> ★★★★★ *"**Many of the students who were asked just to do one piece did not even complete one
> piece. Why? THEY WERE TRAPPED BY MAKING THE PERFECT THING — because there's no such thing as a
> perfect thing. The better work consistently came from the other group**, who didn't even have the
> criteria of making perfect."*

> ★★★★★ **And he turns it on his own training:** *"**I'm a designer, classically trained. This is
> what they emphasise every single day into our brain: quality, quality, quality. BUT LUCKILY, I
> DON'T LISTEN SO GOOD. No — I'M GOING TO PUT IN MY REPS.**"*

> ★★★★★ **The related claim, stated as fact about creative work:** *"if you're a creative person you
> always say: '**if the client gave me more time, it would be better.**' **THAT'S A LIE YOU TELL
> YOURSELF EVERY SINGLE DAY. Because CREATIVITY EXPANDS OR CONTRACTS TO MEET A DEADLINE. That's how
> creativity works. Everything else is a lie or an excuse.**"*

**Stacked quotes:** *"repetition is the mother of skill"* (**Tony Robbins**) · *"you are what you
repeatedly do"* · *"how you do one thing is how you do everything"* → ★★★★ **his own addition**:
*"**and skill is the foundation of confidence.**"* → *"**it's the wish I would have for all of you:
to be more confident in yourselves, in your decision-making, and in who you show up to be
online.**"*

**Jim Rohn**, *"one of my favourite people in the world"*: *"**what you become in the pursuit of
your goals is more important than the accomplishment itself.**"* → *"**it's the pursuit and not the
goal itself.**"*

### ★★★★★ The three circles — the napkin in Las Vegas

At a technology conference, meeting a former student who says: *"**Chris, I noticed something about
you. You seem really happy. Like you're almost glowing right now.**"*

> ★★★★★ *"'You know what? **I think I've figured out the secret to my life.**' And I take out a
> little napkin… **I drew three circles. I think I finally figured out my life IN MY 40s.**
> **Something I love to do. Something I'm really good at. And what the world needs.**"*

**Circle by circle, with the biography attached:**

- ★★★★★ **Teaching — the love, and the exploitation.** *"**for 15 years I taught at Art Center. It
  filled my soul with joy. It recharged me all the way. But when I was teaching at school, THEY
  PAID ME \$58 AN HOUR. That was less money than I paid freelancers to work for me.** So what they
  were doing is **tapping into my passion for teaching and saying: we'll pay you with passion. As
  the entrepreneur I'm like: hm, THAT SOUNDS LIKE EXPLOITATION. And I did it for 15 years.**"*
  > ★★★★★ **And the retort to a criticism he clearly receives:** *"**So if you want to criticise me
  > for pursuing the money — you do something for 15 years where you don't get paid, and tell me how
  > you like it.**"*
- ★★★★★ **The production company — the money, and the leash.** *"**I made a lot of money. I made
  millions of dollars. Actually, I made a couple million dollars BEFORE I TURNED 25.** But **the
  corporate overlords always told me what to do. 'Make it bigger.' 'That pixel — can you move it one
  pixel to the left?' 'We don't like that shade of green, can you make it more emerald?'**"*
- ★★★★★ **2014–15, the merge.** *"a former classmate invited me to make content on YouTube… **I
  finally found something where I could merge all of the things that I love. Entrepreneurship. I
  love reading. I love teaching. I love drawing. I like comic books. I consume pop culture, art,
  history, design, literature — everything. I GET TO COMBINE EVERYTHING ABOUT ME and put it out
  into the world, and I'll figure out a way to make money.**"*

> ★★★★★ **The circle that is deliberately absent:** *"**There's a reason why no circle exists on
> here that says MAKE MONEY. Because I believe when you find that sweet spot in the middle of all
> these things, THE MONEY COMES. I suggest to you right now: IF THE MONEY HASN'T COME, IT'S BECAUSE
> ONE OF THESE CIRCLES IS MISSING — OR IT'S NOT COMPLETE. And I encourage you to investigate
> that.**"*

> ⚠️ **Related to but distinct from the ikigai four-column exercise** at
> [[../topics/branding/authenticity-gap|the authenticity gap]] — **three circles here, four columns
> there, and he does not name ikigai in this telling.** Keep them separate.

### ★★★★★ The gift economy — flagged as a work in progress

> *"**The attention economy, as some people would argue, IS the economy. Whoever can control
> attention controls opportunities.**"*

> ★★★★★ *"**Underneath the attention economy is the GIFT, OR GIVING, ECONOMY.** Bear with me — **it's
> a concept. I'M WORKING ON IT.** If you don't like it, again, no refunds."*

**Its principle:** *"**you give freely with no short-term expectations, where all you're doing is to
build relationships, community, and strengthen social bonds. No money — we're doing it because it
feels good to give.**"*

> ★★★★★ **The exchange:** *"**in lieu of getting money, you trade money for TIME — their time, their
> attention. Perhaps some STATUS, because you're the educator, the thought leader. Maybe SUPPORT…
> and CONNECTION. So instead of getting money, YOU'RE GETTING SOCIAL CURRENCY.**"*

**Four creators offered as examples** — a karaoke-rigged rideshare driver, a lawn-mowing channel
that clears badly overgrown yards for elderly people **free and with no strings attached**, a subway
portrait artist, and a repair creator — all third parties, recorded as context only, and one of them
hedged by him (*"I'm not sure about all these facts"*).

> ★★★★★ **And he pre-empts his own contradiction on the first:** *"'**Wait a minute, Chris — are you
> arguing against yourself now? Isn't that viral?**' **IT IS VIRAL — AS A BYPRODUCT OF HIM BEING
> FULLY ALIGNED WITH WHO HE IS.**"* ✅ **This is the qualifier the whole talk turns on.**

★★★★ **The Simona story**, which is the method applied to a stranger: a chauffeur who is also a
hairstylist and speaks twelve languages, six fluently, and owns both a car business and salons.
*"**What she was telling me sounded like THE OLD ME — the old me who did different things to satisfy
different needs, versus ONE thing that put them all together.**"* His suggestion: a mobile colouring
service filmed in communities whose languages she speaks → *"**yeah, it's a \$300 colour job — but
it's a \$3,000 GIVING ACT.**"* → *"eventually you can do this where **you charge nothing**, and you
just go find really interesting people who are transforming when they see themselves
differently."*

### ★★★★★ Trust at scale — Frei and Morriss

Citing **Frances Frei and Anne Morriss** (*"Begin with Trust"*), three requirements:

1. ★★★★ **Authenticity** — *"**are you who you pretend to be online? So we have to close the
   authenticity gap. Stop performing. Stop becoming a character or an avatar of yourself. Be the
   real you. Share real opinions and ideas.**"*
2. ★★★★★ **Empathy** — *"**not empathy the way you think. WE NEED TO FEEL LIKE YOU CARE ABOUT YOUR
   AUDIENCE MORE THAN YOUR NEED TO MAKE MONEY.**"* (car and timeshare salespeople as the
   counter-example: *"we know what they want — **they want to meet their numbers more than they
   want to take care of us**"*).
3. ★★★★ **Logic** — *"**you have to be able to explain ideas in ways that are easy to follow.**"* →
   ★★★★★ *"**So the pursuit for you is to become a better educator. And my saying is this: IF YOU
   WANT TO REACH MORE PEOPLE, TEACH MORE PEOPLE.**"*

⚠️ He also quotes **Alex Hormozi**: *"you cannot wish for both strong character and an easy life —
the price of each is the other."* **Third-party quotation; never source material for that clone.**

### ★★★★★ The full-circle numbers — and the qualifier

> *"It looks like I'm antiviral and like I don't want to be successful at all. **I started a company,
> a YouTube channel called The Futur, in 2014.**"*

| | |
|---|---|
| Videos made | *"**over 2,000**"* |
| Revenue | *"**over a million dollars**"* |
| Videos past a million views | *"**24**"* |
| Top video | *"**4 and a half million views**"* — with two shorts recuts at **50M** and **44M** |

> ★★★★★ **The qualifier that governs the entire talk:** *"**So there's NOTHING WRONG WITH GOING
> VIRAL. That should not be the GOAL, though.**"*

> ★★★★★ *"**Look at the gap between when we released it and when we went viral. WE ARE THE CLASSIC
> EXAMPLE OF AN OVERNIGHT SUCCESS STORY 10 YEARS IN THE MAKING.**"*

★★★★★ **And a generous credit to the man he spent the hour rebutting:** of the *Man on the Street*
format — *"where I go up to people and ask them: **how much do you charge an hour?** — **and do you
know who told me to say that? MR. BRENDAN KANE told me to say that.**"*

> ★★★★★ **The closing:** *"**The person who loves walking will walk further than the person who loves
> the destination.** … **Learn to love walking. Take that step, and keep taking that step — because
> one day when you look up, you'll be surprised how far you've come.**"*

## ⚠️ What this does to the standing algorithm contradiction

**This is the most important source yet on that thread, and it moves it — without resolving it.**

- His anti-metrics position is here at its **most principled and specific**: not indifference, but
  a refusal to *"export my emotional well-being to a machine."* That is an argument about
  **self-worth**, not about craft.
- ★★★★★ He states plainly: *"**there's nothing wrong with going viral. That should not be the goal,
  though**"* — and he applies the same distinction to someone else's viral hit: *"**it is viral as a
  byproduct of him being fully aligned with who he is.**"*
- Against that sit three *"student of the game"* statements ([[2026-02-17-yt-nTthfof409U|2026-02-17]],
  [[2026-01-03-yt-meVstiZhvqA|2026-01-03]], 2025-08-23) about **titles, thumbnails and studying what
  works** — a craft-level activity, not a self-worth one.

> ⚠️ **A goal/byproduct reading would reconcile them cleanly. He never states it as a
> reconciliation.** Record the qualifier prominently, note that this source comes closest, **and
> leave the bridge marked as the curator's.**
