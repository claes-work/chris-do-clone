# External sources sub-index (Phase 4 — everything that is not a YouTube upload)

Sister file to [`youtube-index.md`](youtube-index.md). Covers **guest appearances on other people's
shows, press interviews, articles and web material**. Ledger ids use the `ext-` prefix; the one
YouTube-hosted external appearance keeps the `yt-` prefix so the caption tooling still works.

> ⚠️ **Why this tier is worth the effort.** On his own channel Chris **teaches**; as someone else's
> guest he is **asked** — about origins, mistakes, money and identity. The first source ingested here
> supplied more biography in one page than most weeks of video did, and it did so because an interviewer
> asked questions his own format never poses.

> ✅ **One structural advantage over the whole video corpus**: printed Q&As mark who is speaking
> **typographically**. After a week of speaker-attribution triage on unlabelled transcripts, these
> sources need **no inference at all**.
